---
title: 'Merging and duplicates'
description: 'Combine two contacts that are the same person, and undo a merge.'
---

<Note>
Merging is available once your brand uses [Contacts](/contacts/overview).
</Note>

ElasticFunnels already joins records that share an email address. When the same person shows up as two contacts (for example they bought once with a work email and once with a personal one), you can merge them by hand.

## Merge two contacts
{/* SCREENSHOT: Merge contact modal with a search result · desktop */}

1. Open the contact you want to **keep**.
2. Go to the **Merges** tab and click **Merge a duplicate**.
3. Search for the duplicate by name or email.
4. Click **Merge** next to the right result.

The contact you opened stays. The other one is folded into it:

- Its emails and phones are added to the contact you kept.
- Its consent history and tags move over, and its orders count toward the contact you kept once the order totals are next recalculated.
- Where the contact you kept has a field filled in, that value stays. Where it is empty, the value comes from the merged contact.
- The merged contact disappears from the list. An old link to it opens the contact you kept.

## Undo a merge
{/* SCREENSHOT: Merges tab with an Undo button · desktop */}

On the **Merges** tab, every merge shows when it happened and until when it can be undone. Click **Undo** to split the two contacts again: the merged contact comes back with its own emails, phones and history.

A merge can be undone for 180 days. After that the **Undo** button is gone and the merge is permanent.

## See who a contact matches
{/* SCREENSHOT: Matches block in the contact side panel · desktop */}

The **Matches** block in a contact's side panel lists the records tied to that person. The same block appears in an order's details, under **Customer information**, for the person who placed the order.

- **Merged contacts**: how many contacts were merged into this one and when the latest merge happened. **View merges** opens the Merges tab, where each merge can be undone.
- **Possible duplicates**: contacts in this brand that look like the same person, with the reason (same phone, same visitor profile, a shared order). Click **Open** to check one, then merge it from the Merges tab if it is the same person.
- **Other brands**: contacts with the same email address or phone number in other brands of your organization. Seeing a match here merges nothing.

Each line has a label. **Confirmed** means the same email or phone, or a merge someone on your team made. **Likely** means a hint that a person should check before merging. Hover a label to see what it means.

Matches only look inside your organization. Brands of another organization are never checked, even when they belong to the same person. If the match is in a brand of your organization that you can't open, you see only a count, with no brand name, contact or link. Contact names from another brand are masked unless you can see unmasked contact details in that brand.

To build an audience from these matches (open duplicates, recent merges, people who are also contacts in your other brands), use the [identity match rule](/analytics/audience-identity-matches).

## Who can merge

Merging and undoing need permission to edit customers. To see the emails of the contacts you are merging, you also need the **See unmasked** permission on the **Contact details** row; without it the search results are masked.
