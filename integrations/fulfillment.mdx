## Overview
{/* SCREENSHOT: Overview · integration settings or log state · desktop */}

The fulfillment integrations push completed orders to a 3PL (Third-Party Logistics) provider and pull cost-of-goods (COGS) data back so your conversions show real profit, not just revenue.

## How fulfillment dispatch works
{/* SCREENSHOT: How It Works · integration settings or log state · desktop */}

1. **Order received** — A customer completes a purchase on your funnel.
2. **Merchant resolution** — The merchant on the conversion is looked up.
3. **Fulfillment provider lookup** — The product's `fulfillment_provider_id` (or merchant default) decides where the order goes. Each product can route to a different provider.
4. **Job queued** — A fulfillment job is queued ~10 seconds after the conversion is finalized so refunds in the first window can short-circuit it.
5. **Order pushed** — Customer info, shipping address, line items (with mapped SKUs), shipping method, and order total are sent to the provider.
6. **Tracking sync** — When the provider returns shipment status and tracking, EF stores it on the conversion and surfaces it in the dashboard. If the order was paid through a [PayPal](/merchants/paypal#tracking-numbers-are-sent-to-paypal) merchant, the tracking number is also sent to PayPal.

<Note>
Shipping from Shopify instead? With [Shopify Order Sync](/integrations/shopify-order-sync#tracking-back-from-shopify), the tracking your store or its 3PL app adds in Shopify comes back to ElasticFunnels too, and is sent to PayPal for PayPal-paid orders.
</Note>

## Send from an automation
{/* SCREENSHOT: Send to fulfillment step in the automation builder · desktop */}

You can also send orders from an automation that starts with **On New Purchase**: add the **Send to fulfillment** step, pick the fulfillment integration and, if you want, a **Delay (minutes)** (empty uses the integration's delay). Put conditions or a split test before it to choose which orders go where. The builder will not save the step on any other trigger, or without a connected fulfillment integration. The **Presets** tab of the fulfillment integration has a ready one: **New purchase: send the order to ... to ship**, and the **Automations** panel at the top of its page shows how many automations send to it.

A fulfillment integration is not moved to automations the way a call center is: which orders it ships follows your merchants and products, and one automation cannot copy that exactly. Keep the merchant and product settings, and use the step for the orders you want to route yourself.

The step runs the same order job as the merchant setting: only physical items, never test orders, gift orders wait for their main payment, and the integration's SKU map and tracking sync apply.

<Warning>
An order is never sent twice. While an active automation sends orders to a fulfillment integration, the merchant's default and product routing stop sending orders to that integration, and an order that is already scheduled is never scheduled again.
</Warning>

## Setup — ShipOffers
{/* SCREENSHOT: ShipOffers setup · integration settings · desktop */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get credentials">
    Contact your ShipOffers account manager and request your **Store ID**, **Username**, and **Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the integration">
    Go to **Integrations** → **Operations** → **ShipOffers Fulfillment**. Enter the credentials and pick a default shipping service (Standard, Express, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map SKUs">
    In ShipOffers, set up your products and copy the SKU codes. In ElasticFunnels, edit each product and ensure its SKU matches the ShipOffers SKU exactly. SKU is what the picker uses to fulfill — mismatched SKUs cause picking errors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign to merchants or products">
    Either set ShipOffers as the **Fulfillment Provider** on a merchant (default for all products under that merchant) or override per product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="(Optional) Enable COGS reconciliation">
    Activate **ShipOffers COGS Reconciliation** as a separate integration, copy the dedicated reconciliation email address, and ask ShipOffers to email weekly cost CSVs to that address. EF parses each CSV and updates the matched conversions with line-item costs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setup — Jetpack
{/* SCREENSHOT: Jetpack setup · integration settings · desktop */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get credentials">
    Contact Jetpack at jetpackshipping.com or 1-844-4-JETPACK. You'll receive an **API Key ID** and **Secret Key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the integration">
    Go to **Integrations** → **Operations** → **Jetpack Fulfillment**. Enter your credentials and configure the primary warehouse and shipping preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map products and SKUs">
    Set up your product catalog inside Jetpack with the same SKU codes you use on each `BrandProduct` in EF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign to merchants or products">
    Set Jetpack as the **Fulfillment Provider** at the merchant level, or override per product when only some products ship from Jetpack.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## SKU mapping
{/* SCREENSHOT: SKU mapping · product detail · desktop */}

The SKU on every `BrandProduct` in EF must match the SKU your 3PL uses to identify the physical item. Without a match, the order will be rejected by the provider or filled with the wrong product. To set or update the SKU:

1. Go to **Products**.
2. Edit the product.
3. Set its **SKU** to the value from your 3PL catalog.
4. Save.

Re-saving a product does **not** retroactively re-fulfill old orders; only new orders use the updated SKU.

## Monitoring
{/* SCREENSHOT: Monitoring · fulfillment list · desktop */}

Go to **Conversions → Fulfillment** to see every fulfillment attempt with its status:

- **Pending** — Queued, waiting for the dispatch window to elapse.
- **Sent** — Provider accepted the order.
- **Failed** — Provider rejected the payload (most often: missing SKU or invalid address). Use the failure detail to fix the data and retry.
- **Tracking** — Provider has shipped the order; the tracking number and carrier are surfaced inline.

<Info>
If a fulfillment attempt fails because of a bad address, you can manually edit the conversion's shipping address and re-send. SKU mismatches require fixing the product first; orders already in flight are not re-keyed automatically.
</Info>
