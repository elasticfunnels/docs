## Overview
{/* SCREENSHOT: Overview · integration settings or log state · desktop */}

The Google Ads integration syncs ad spend into ElasticFunnels and sends conversion events back to Google. Use it to compare campaign cost with funnel revenue and feed better conversion data to Google Ads.

## Setup Guide
{/* SCREENSHOT: Setup Guide · integration settings or log state · desktop */}

### Step 1: Connect Your Google Ads Account

1. Navigate to **Settings → Integrations → Advertising**
2. Find **Google Ads** and click **"New integration"** or **"Manage integrations"**
3. Click **"Connect your Google Ads Account"**
4. You'll be redirected to Google to authorize access
5. Sign in with the Google account that has access to your Google Ads account
6. On the permission screen, **leave every box ticked**, including the Google Ads box

<Warning>
**Do not untick the Google Ads permission.** Google's consent screen lets you approve the sign-in while refusing the Google Ads permission, and the connection still completes. It looks connected, but no ad accounts can be read and no spend imports. See [Connection and Permissions](#connection-and-permissions) for how to spot and fix this.
</Warning>

<Info>
You'll need admin access to your Google Ads account to complete the authorization. A Google Ads Developer Token must be configured at the application level (contact support if needed).
</Info>

### Step 2: Select Your Ad Account

After authorization, you'll see a list of all Google Ads accounts you have access to:

1. Review the available accounts (shown with account ID and name)
2. Select the account you want to track
3. The selected account will be saved to your integration

<Tip>
If you manage multiple businesses, you can create separate integrations for each Google Ads account to keep reporting organized.
</Tip>

If the list comes back empty or shows a red error box, the connection has a problem that selecting an account will not fix. Read [When No Ad Accounts Appear](#when-no-ad-accounts-appear).

### Step 3: Configure Conversion Tracking (Required for Postbacks)

To enable conversion postbacks (highly recommended):

1. **Enter your Conversion Action ID**
   - Find it in Google Ads → Tools & Settings → Conversions
   - Create a new conversion action if you don't have one
   - Copy the Conversion Action ID (numeric value)
   
2. **Enable Conversion Postbacks**
   - Check the box to enable automatic postback sending
   - This sends purchase data back to Google Ads when customers complete orders

3. Click **"Update Integration"** to save

<Warning>
Conversion postbacks require a valid Conversion Action ID. If you enable postbacks without a Conversion Action ID, the system will warn you. The postback will only fire when conversions have a valid gclid (Google Click ID) parameter.
</Warning>

## Connection and Permissions
{/* SCREENSHOT: Connected Google Account · integration settings showing the connected account card and the Reconnect button · desktop */}

### Which Google Account Is Connected

The integration stores credentials for one Google account, and that account is not always the person who set it up. An agency staffer, a former employee, or a personal Gmail can end up holding the connection.

Open the integration and look at the **Connected Google Account** card at the top. It shows:

- The email address of the Google account that authorized the connection
- The account name, when Google returns one different from the email
- The date the connection was made

<Tip>
Check this card first whenever spend stops importing. If the email is not the account you expect, the connection is reading a different set of Google Ads accounts than you think.
</Tip>

If the card says the account has not been recorded yet, the integration was connected before this was tracked. Click **Reconnect** and the email will be filled in.

### The Google Ads Permission

The connection asks Google for three things: your email, your basic profile, and access to Google Ads. Only the third one lets ElasticFunnels read campaigns and spend.

Google's consent screen shows these as separate tick boxes. Approving the sign-in while unticking the Google Ads box produces a working set of credentials with no Google Ads access. The connection saves and reports success. Every later request to Google Ads is refused.

When this happens the integration shows a red banner:

> **Google Ads permission was not granted.** This account is signed in, but it did not approve access to Google Ads, so no ad accounts can be read and spend will not import. Reconnect and leave the Google Ads box ticked on the consent screen.

The fix is to reconnect and approve the Google Ads permission.

### Reconnecting an Integration

Use the **Reconnect** button on the integration you want to fix, not the generic connect button on the Advertising tab.

The Reconnect button names the integration it belongs to, so the new credentials replace that integration's credentials. Starting a fresh connect flow on a brand that already has several Google Ads integrations can write the new credentials onto a different one.

<Info>
Reconnecting always shows Google's permission screen again, even for an account you have already approved. This is deliberate. It is the only way to grant a permission you previously refused.
</Info>

Reconnecting keeps your selected ad account, Conversion Action ID, and postback settings. It replaces only the credentials.

## When No Ad Accounts Appear
{/* SCREENSHOT: Ad account lookup failure · integration settings showing the red lookup error and Reconnect button · desktop */}

An empty ad account list is a symptom, not a cause. The integration reports what Google actually said, so read the message shown in the red box rather than guessing.

| What the integration shows | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **No Google account is connected** | The integration row exists but no connect flow ever finished. | Click **Connect your Google Ads Account**. |
| **Google refused access to Google Ads for this account** | The credentials are valid, but they lack the Google Ads permission. | Reconnect and leave the Google Ads box ticked. |
| **Access to this Google account was revoked** | Someone removed ElasticFunnels in their Google account security settings, or the password changed. | Reconnect. |
| **Could not sign in to Google with the stored credentials** | Google rejected the stored credentials when refreshing them. | Reconnect. |
| **Google rejected the stored credentials** | The credentials are no longer accepted. | Reconnect. |
| **The Google Ads API version in use has been retired** | A platform-level problem, not an account problem. | Contact support. Reconnecting will not help. |
| **No ad accounts found** with no error | Google answered normally and the account genuinely has no Google Ads accounts. | Confirm the connected email has access to a Google Ads account. |

<Warning>
The most common cause of a silently broken Google Ads integration is the refused Google Ads permission. It is easy to miss because the connection reports success, the integration looks configured, and the only symptom is that no data ever arrives.
</Warning>

## Understanding Google Click ID (gclid)
{/* SCREENSHOT: Understanding Google Click ID (gclid) · integration settings or log state · desktop */}

### How It Works

Google Ads uses **gclid** (Google Click ID) to track which clicks lead to conversions:

1. When someone clicks your Google Ads link, Google adds a `gclid` parameter to the URL
2. ElasticFunnels automatically captures this `gclid` in the click tracking data
3. When a conversion occurs, we send the `gclid` back to Google Ads
4. Google Ads attributes the conversion to the original click

### Why This Matters

```
Example: Conversion Attribution

User clicks Google Ad → URL includes gclid=abc123
User visits your funnel → gclid is captured
User makes purchase → Conversion postback sent with gclid=abc123
Google Ads → Attributes conversion to original click
```

<Check>
The gclid is automatically captured when users click Google Ads links. You don't need to manually configure anything - just ensure you're using Google Ads tracking links.
</Check>

Your ad spend data syncs automatically on a regular schedule. This includes:

- Daily spend amounts
- Click counts

Historical data is available immediately after connecting. Future data syncs automatically without any action needed.

3. View spend, clicks, and ROI metrics
4. Filter by campaign, ad group, or specific ads

## Conversion Postbacks
{/* SCREENSHOT: Conversion Postbacks · integration settings or log state · desktop */}

### What Are Conversion Postbacks?

Conversion postbacks send purchase information back to Google Ads when a customer completes an order. This helps Google Ads:

- Optimize your campaigns for conversions (not just clicks)
- Improve audience targeting based on who actually buys
- Provide accurate attribution data
- Enable features like automated bidding

### What Data is Sent

When a conversion occurs, we send:

- Purchase event
- Order value and currency
- Order ID
- Google Click ID (gclid)
- Conversion Action ID

<Info>
All customer data is handled according to Google's privacy requirements. Conversion data is sent securely to Google Ads.
</Info>

### When Postbacks Are Triggered

Postbacks fire automatically when:

1. ✅ Customer clicked a Google Ad (gclid present in click data)
2. ✅ Customer completed a purchase
3. ✅ Integration has postbacks enabled
4. ✅ Conversion Action ID is configured

<Warning>
If a conversion doesn't have a gclid (e.g., user didn't come from Google Ads), no postback will be sent. This is expected behavior.
</Warning>

## Tracking Link Builder Integration
{/* SCREENSHOT: Tracking Link Builder Integration · integration settings or log state · desktop */}

The Tracking Link Builder automatically generates the correct UTM parameters for Google Ads:

**Google Ads Template:**
```
utm_source=google
utm_medium=cpc
utm_campaign={campaignid}
utm_term={keyword}
utm_content={creative}
adid=ga_{creative}_{campaignid}
```

<Info>
Access the Tracking Link Builder from **Tools → Tracking → Tracking Link Builder** or from the Pages/Funnels list dropdown menu.
</Info>

## Campaign Management
{/* SCREENSHOT: Campaign Management · integration settings or log state · desktop */}

Go to **Reports → Ad Campaigns** in the sidebar and pick your Google Ads integration to browse the account:

- Campaigns, with spend, clicks, and impressions for a date range you choose
- Ad groups inside each campaign
- Ads inside each ad group, including headlines and descriptions
- Funnel revenue and profit next to the ad metrics, when your tracking links carry the right parameters

The status column accounts for parents. An ad group that is enabled inside a paused campaign reads as **Active but campaign paused**, so you can tell the difference between an ad group you paused and one that cannot deliver because of the campaign above it.

See the [Campaigns Management](/integrations/campaigns-management) guide for the full interface, metric definitions, and the tracking link requirements that make funnel metrics appear.

## Managing Multiple Integrations
{/* SCREENSHOT: Managing Multiple Integrations · integration settings or log state · desktop */}

If you manage multiple businesses or clients, you can create multiple Google Ads integrations:

### When to Create Multiple Integrations

- Managing different client accounts (agencies)
- Separate businesses with different Google Ads accounts
- Different brands under one ElasticFunnels account
- Testing vs. production campaigns

### How It Works

Each integration is independent:
- Has its own Google Ads account
- Has its own Conversion Action ID
- Has its own postback settings
- Syncs separately

<Tip>
Name each integration clearly (e.g., "Client A - Supplements", "Client B - Software") to keep them organized.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting
{/* SCREENSHOT: Troubleshooting · integration settings or log state · desktop */}

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No Data Appearing After Setup">
    **Possible Causes:**
    - The Google Ads permission was refused on the consent screen (most common)
    - First sync hasn't run yet
    - No ad spend in the selected date range
    - Ad account has no active campaigns

    **Solution:** Open the integration and check the **Connected Google Account** card. If a red permission banner is showing, reconnect and leave the Google Ads box ticked. Otherwise wait for the first sync, or contact support to trigger a manual sync.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Ad Account List Is Empty">
    **Cause:** Almost always a credentials or permission problem rather than a missing account.

    **Solution:** Read the message in the red box on the integration and match it against the table in [When No Ad Accounts Appear](#when-no-ad-accounts-appear). Most cases are fixed by reconnecting.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wrong Google Account Is Connected">
    **Cause:** The connection was made by someone else, or with a different Google login than intended. Agencies hit this when a staff member connects with their own account.

    **Solution:** Check the email on the **Connected Google Account** card. Click **Reconnect**, sign in as the correct Google account, and approve the Google Ads permission. Your ad account selection and postback settings are kept.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Session Expired / Connection Error">
    **Cause:** The stored Google credentials stopped working. This happens when the permission is removed in the Google account's security settings, when the password changes, or when Google revokes access.

    **Solution:** Click **Reconnect** on your integration and approve the Google Ads permission again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An Ad Group or Ad Shows Active but Gets No Traffic">
    **Cause:** A parent is paused. An enabled ad group inside a paused campaign cannot deliver, and neither can an enabled ad inside a paused ad group.

    **Solution:** The status column names the reason, for example **Active but campaign paused**. Activate the parent, or accept that the child will not run until you do.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Conversions Not Showing in Google Ads">
    **Possible Causes:**
    - Postbacks not enabled
    - No Conversion Action ID configured
    - Customer didn't click a Google Ad (no gclid)
    - gclid expired (Google Ads has a 90-day attribution window)
    
    **Solution:** 
    1. Verify Conversion Action ID is entered correctly
    2. Check "Enable Conversion Postbacks" is checked
    3. Ensure customers are clicking Google Ads links (gclid must be present)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a Developer Token?">
    **Answer:** A Developer Token is required for Google Ads API access. It's configured at the application level by ElasticFunnels support, not per integration. If you see errors about Developer Token, contact support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
