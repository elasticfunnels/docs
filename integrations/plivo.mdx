## Overview

Plivo can be the carrier behind your brand's texts. Once you connect a Plivo account and add one of its
numbers as a sender, texts from the [Send SMS step](/automations/send-sms), test sends and the automatic
STOP, HELP and START replies go out through Plivo. They pass the same checks as every other text first
(suppression list, quiet hours, consent, state rules), set up in [Settings -> SMS](/settings/sms).

Plivo has no automation steps of its own. It is a sender, like a Twilio number. You pay Plivo directly
for every text, and ElasticFunnels adds nothing on top.

Only US numbers can be texted for now.

## What you need in Plivo

- **A Plivo account** with credit on it. A Plivo trial account can only text numbers you have verified in
  the Plivo console.
- **A number that can send SMS, or a Powerpack.** You can use any of these:

| Sender | What Plivo needs before carriers accept your texts |
|---|---|
| Local number (10DLC) | Your brand and a 10DLC campaign registered in Plivo, with the number linked to the campaign |
| Toll-free number | Toll-free verification approved in Plivo |
| Powerpack (a pool of numbers) | The numbers in it registered as above |

- **Your Auth ID and Auth Token.** Both are on the overview page of the
  [Plivo console](https://console.plivo.com/dashboard/). The Auth ID is 20 characters and starts with `MA`
  (or `SA` for a subaccount).

## Connect Plivo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plivo in Integrations">
    Go to **Apps** -> **Integrations** and open **Plivo**. Click **Add connection**.

    <img src="/images/integrations/plivo-connect.png" alt="The Plivo connection form: Name, Auth ID and Auth Token, with where to find them in the Plivo console" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your credentials">
    Give the connection a **Name** (for example "Plivo main account"), then paste the **Auth ID** and the
    **Auth Token** from the Plivo console. Save.

    The Auth Token is stored encrypted. ElasticFunnels also uses it to check that replies and delivery
    reports really come from Plivo, so keep it current here if you ever change it in Plivo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a sender">
    Open **Settings** -> **SMS** -> **Senders** and click **Add sender**. Choose your Plivo connection under
    **SMS provider account**, then pick a **Number**. The list shows the SMS-enabled numbers and the
    Powerpacks on that account; a Powerpack shows as a messaging service.

    Turn on **Default sender** if this is the number a step should use when it does not pick one. Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Configure webhooks">
    In the sender's row, click **Configure webhooks**. ElasticFunnels sets up Plivo to send replies to it.
    See [Replies and delivery reports](#replies-and-delivery-reports) for what this changes in your Plivo
    account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the registration">
    Click **Refresh** in the **Registration** column. ElasticFunnels reads the 10DLC status (local numbers)
    or the toll-free verification status from Plivo and shows **Registered**, **Pending**, **Rejected** or
    **Unknown**. A Powerpack stays at **Unknown**, so check its numbers in Plivo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the consent statement">
    On the **Compliance** tab, accept the marketing consent statement. Marketing texts are skipped until
    someone on the brand does. See [SMS compliance](/settings/sms#marketing-consent-statement).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The sender form has a switch labelled **Twilio already answers STOP/HELP for this number**. For a Plivo
number it means Plivo: turn it on only if your Plivo account already sends its own STOP and HELP replies for
that number, so the person does not get two replies. ElasticFunnels then records the reply and the
opt-out without sending its own.

## Replies and delivery reports

There is no URL to copy into Plivo. **Configure webhooks** does it for you:

- **Replies (STOP, HELP, START and anything else people text back).** ElasticFunnels creates a Plivo
  application named `elasticfunnels-brand-<your brand ID>` whose message URL points at your brand's
  ElasticFunnels address (it contains `/api/webhook/sms/plivo/`), and attaches it to the number or
  Powerpack. Clicking it again later reuses the same application.
- **Delivery reports.** Every text ElasticFunnels sends carries its own report address, so nothing needs
  to be set in Plivo for them.

<Warning>
Plivo allows one application per number. If the number was attached to another application (for
example one that answers voice calls), **Configure webhooks** replaces it. Use a number that is only for
texting, or check its application in the Plivo console afterwards.
</Warning>

Plivo signs every request it sends with your Auth Token. ElasticFunnels refuses any request that is
unsigned or signed with a different token, and the address also carries a secret token for your brand.

A text's status in **Settings** -> **Logs** -> **SMS** follows what Plivo reports: **Sent**, then
**Delivered**, **Undelivered** or **Failed**. Plivo's "rejected" shows as **Failed**, and "read" as
**Delivered**.

## Send a test

1. Open **Settings** -> **SMS** -> **Test send**.
2. Enter a number you own, pick the Plivo sender, choose the type and write a short message.
3. Send. The tab shows what the checks decided: sent, waiting or skipped, with the reason.
4. Open **Settings** -> **Logs** -> **SMS**. The text should move from **Sent** to **Delivered** within a
   minute or so.
5. Reply **STOP** from your phone. It shows under **Replies**, your number lands on the
   **Suppression list**, and you get the STOP reply. Text **START** to lift it again.

If step 4 stays on **Sent** and step 5 shows nothing, Plivo's requests are not reaching ElasticFunnels:
see the first item under Troubleshooting.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No replies and no delivery reports">
    Click **Configure webhooks** (or **Set again**) on the sender. If you changed the Auth Token in Plivo,
    paste the new one into the Plivo integration first: requests signed with a token ElasticFunnels does
    not have are refused. Also check in the Plivo console that the number still uses the
    `elasticfunnels-brand-...` application.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='"This number is not in the connected Plivo account"'>
    The number was released or moved to another Plivo account or subaccount. Remove the sender and add a
    number from the account the integration uses.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='"Could not reach Plivo" or the number list is empty'>
    The Auth ID or Auth Token is wrong, or Plivo did not answer. Check both on the Plivo console overview
    page and save the integration again. The list shows the first 20 numbers and the first 20 Powerpacks
    on the account, and leaves out numbers without SMS.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Auth ID is refused when saving">
    The Auth ID is exactly 20 letters and digits, starting with MA or SA. Copy it from the console
    overview, not the Auth Token.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A text failed: what the Plivo error means">
    Click the text in **Settings** -> **Logs** -> **SMS** to see Plivo's error code and its meaning.

    | Code | Meaning | What ElasticFunnels does |
    |---|---|---|
    | 200 | The person replied STOP to Plivo | Adds the number to the suppression list |
    | 50, 70 | Invalid number, or no longer in service | Adds the number to the suppression list as invalid |
    | 40 | Invalid sending number | Logs it as a provider error. Check the sender |
    | 900 | Plivo account out of credit | Logs it as a provider error. Top up in Plivo |
    | 910 | Plivo account disabled | Logs it as a provider error. Contact Plivo |
    | 420 | Expired in Plivo's queue | Usually the number is not linked to a 10DLC campaign |
    | 960 | Toll-free number not verified | Finish toll-free verification in Plivo |
    | 30 | Carrier spam filter | Change the wording; see the content warnings in the log |
    | 450 | Country disabled in Plivo | Turn the country on in Plivo's settings |
    | 401 | Plivo refused the credentials | Update the Auth ID and Auth Token |

    A number on the suppression list gets no more texts until the person texts START or you lift it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The log says Twilio for a Plivo text">
    Some reasons in the log are still worded for Twilio, for example "Twilio rejected the text". For a
    Plivo sender they mean Plivo. The error code next to them is Plivo's.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
