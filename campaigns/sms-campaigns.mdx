An SMS campaign sends one text to everyone in the segments you pick. It goes out from a number on your own
Twilio, Plivo or Telnyx account, and each text passes the same checks as every other text your brand sends:
STOP replies, the suppression list, consent, quiet hours and the states you skip.

Open **Campaigns** in the sidebar, click **New campaign**, then **SMS broadcast** ("Text a segment of contacts
who agreed to get SMS."). The composer has five steps: **Setup**, **Audience**, **Message**, **Schedule** and
**Review**. Your draft saves as you go.

<Note>
SMS campaigns are for US numbers at launch. The brand's allowed countries are set to the US. In **Warn** mode a
number outside the US is still texted and flagged with a warning; in **Strict** mode it is skipped.
</Note>

## Before your first campaign

Set these up in **Settings → SMS** (the **SMS** group in the Campaigns menu links to each tab). Full details are
on [SMS compliance](/settings/sms).

1. **SMS on for the brand.** Until it is, you can write a campaign but it cannot go out.
2. **A sender.** Connect your [Twilio](/integrations/twilio), [Plivo](/integrations/plivo) or
   [Telnyx](/integrations/telnyx) account and add a number under **Senders**.
3. **The marketing consent statement.** On the **Compliance** tab, tick "I have read this statement and accept
   it for this brand." and click **Accept statement**. Without it, every marketing text is skipped, whichever
   mode you use.
4. **A link domain**, if your texts have links. Links are blocked until the brand has one.
5. **Contacts.** The audience comes from Analytics segments of your contacts, so contacts must be set up for the
   brand.

## 1. Setup

- **Campaign name**: only you see it.
- **Send from**: pick one of your senders. The default active sender is picked for you.
- **Compliance** shows the mode your brand uses:
  - **Strict mode**: texts that break a rule are not sent.
  - **Warning mode**: texts that break a rule are sent and flagged.

  Change the mode with **Open SMS settings**.

## 2. Audience

<img src="/images/campaigns/sms-audience.png" alt="SMS campaign, Audience step: segments to send to, states to skip and how many people will get the text" />

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Send to** | Everyone in these segments who has a phone number. **Add a segment** (up to 20). |
| **Leave out** | People in these segments never get this text, even if a segment above includes them. |
| **Skip states** | Leave out states with strict texting laws: **Skip by risk level** (**Very high risk**, **High risk**, **Moderate risk** or **None**), or **Skip a state** one by one. Skipped states lock once the campaign starts sending. |

Only contacts who have not opted out of SMS are listed. The **Will get the text** card counts who is left after
ElasticFunnels removes people with no phone number, the same number twice, numbers on the suppression list and
numbers in a skipped state.

How consent is counted depends on your mode:

- **Warn**: people whose consent could not be confirmed are counted as reachable, with a note saying how many.
  Their texts go out with a warning.
- **Strict**: they are left out as **No SMS consent**.

Contacts who opted out, said the number is not theirs, or are suppressed are always left out.

## 3. Message

<img src="/images/campaigns/sms-message.png" alt="SMS campaign, Message step: text, character and part count, opt-out line, variants and phone preview" />

Write the text in **Message** (up to 1,600 characters). Use `{first_name|there}` to add the person's first
name, with "there" when it is missing.

The counter under the box shows characters and parts. One part holds 160 standard characters, or 153 each when
the text runs to several parts. An emoji or special character switches the text to **Unicode**, which cuts each
part to 70 characters (67 when split), so the text costs more.

**Add opt-out line** is on by default and adds the **Footer** ("Reply STOP to opt out") on a new line after
every text. You can change the wording, up to 80 characters, but it must keep the word STOP. If your message
already says STOP, the footer is not added twice. Turn the switch off and the review step warns that carriers
may filter the texts more often; you can still send.

**Links.** Each link in the text becomes a short link on your link domain (`https://your-domain/s/code`), one per
person, tagged with `utm_source=sms`, `utm_medium=sms` and `utm_campaign=sms-(campaign id)`. That is how the
report ties clicks and orders to the campaign. Under **Link preview**, set the **Preview title** and **Preview
image URL** that phones show for the link.

**Variants.** Click **Add a variant** to test up to four more wordings. **Test group size (%)** (20% by default)
gets all the versions; after **Pick the winner after** (60 minutes by default), the winner goes to everyone
else. How the winner is scored: [A/B testing a campaign](/campaigns/ab-testing).

**Send a test to** a phone number and click **Send test**. The test passes the same compliance checks as the
real send.

## 4. Schedule

<img src="/images/campaigns/sms-schedule.png" alt="SMS campaign, Schedule step: send time, spreading the sends, days and hours, and the quiet hours note" />

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **When** | **Send now**, or **Send later** with a date and time |
| **Spread the sends over** | **Send all at once** (the default), or spread evenly over 30 minutes, 1, 2, 4 or 8 hours |
| **Days and hours** | Texts only go out on these days, between **From** and **To** in each person's time zone. Outside these hours the text waits. |

Quiet hours from your SMS settings apply on top of the campaign's own hours. With them on, a text that falls
outside the allowed window waits until the morning in the person's time zone (8am to 8pm by default, with
stricter rules in states such as Pennsylvania and Connecticut). A text that has waited 48 hours is skipped. With
quiet hours off, the text goes out and is flagged with a warning.

## 5. Review

<img src="/images/campaigns/sms-review.png" alt="SMS campaign, Review step: summary, the before you send checklist and the Launch button" />

**Summary** repeats the sender, audience, message and schedule, each with **Edit**. **Before you send** checks:

- SMS is on for this brand
- An active sender is picked
- The audience has people to text
- The message is written
- Links have a link domain
- The send time is valid

Click **Launch** (or **Schedule**) and confirm. The dialog says how many people will get the text. Texts cannot
be unsent.

<Warning>
In **Warn** mode the checklist does not list the marketing consent statement, but launch still fails without
it. Accept the statement in **Settings → SMS → Compliance** before you launch.
</Warning>

## The report

<img src="/images/campaigns/sms-report.png" alt="SMS campaign report, Overview: recipients, sent, delivered, failed, clicked and revenue, with skipped and warnings" />

The report refreshes on its own while the campaign is scheduled or sending. The cards show **Recipients**,
**Sent**, **Delivered**, **Failed**, **Clicked** (unique clicks) and **Revenue**. The tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | The texts that were **Skipped** and the **Warnings**, each with the reason, and the message |
| **Recipients** | Each person (phone number masked), status, state, test group, clicks and send time |
| **Variants** | Each version's sends, clicks, orders and revenue, and the **Winner** |
| **Links** | Each link and its clicks |
| **Revenue** | Revenue, cost and return |

<img src="/images/campaigns/sms-report-revenue.png" alt="SMS campaign report, Revenue tab: revenue from clicks, orders after the send, cost and return" />

Revenue is counted two ways and kept apart:

- **From clicks**: orders by people who clicked a link in this text in the 7 days before they bought, or orders
  that carry the campaign's `utm_campaign`.
- **After the send**: orders within 24 hours of the text by people who did not click. Other channels may also
  have reached them, so this is never added to **From clicks**.

Both are net of refunds and chargebacks. **Cost** is what your provider reports for the texts, in USD, and
**Return** compares revenue with it. There is no cost estimate before you launch.

<img src="/images/campaigns/sms-report-variants.png" alt="SMS campaign report, Variants tab: each text with sends, clicks, click rate, orders and revenue, and the winner" />

### Pause, stop and duplicate

| Button | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Edit** | Draft or scheduled | Opens the composer |
| **Unschedule** | Scheduled | Turns it back into a draft |
| **Pause** / **Resume** | Sending or paused | **Pause** holds the texts not sent yet; **Resume** sends them |
| **Stop sending** | Scheduled, sending or paused | Texts not sent yet will not go out. Texts already sent stay sent. |
| **Duplicate** | Any time | Opens a copy in the composer |

A sending campaign pauses on its own after 10 carrier rejections in a row or an error from your provider
account. Check your sender and provider, then click **Resume**.

## STOP and opt-outs

When someone replies STOP (or STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT and similar words), their number goes on
the brand's SMS suppression list, the opt-out is logged, and they get one confirmation reply. START takes them
off the list. Suppressed numbers are left out when the campaign starts and checked again as each text goes out.
More on the automatic replies: [SMS compliance](/settings/sms#automatic-replies).
