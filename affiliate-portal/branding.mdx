The portal's **Branding** tab sets how the portal looks to affiliates and how its emails read. **Live preview**,
beside the fields, shows an approximate result before you save; **Open in new tab** shows the real portal.

<img src="/images/affiliate-portal/branding.png" alt="The Branding tab with colors, logos, fonts, copy and links, and the live preview of the portal" />

## Look

| Section | Fields |
|---|---|
| **Visual identity** | Primary, Accent, Background, Text, Header background and Header text colors. Leave one empty to keep the default. |
| **Logos and images** | Logo for light backgrounds, logo for dark backgrounds, Favicon, and the image beside the sign-in form. |
| **Typography** | Body and heading font family. For Google Fonts, also paste the font's `<link>` tag into **Custom HTML (head)**. |
| **Copy and labels** | Header title, Footer text, and the Login subheading under the sign-in title. |
| **Support and legal links** | Support, Terms and Privacy URLs, shown to affiliates on the portal. |
| **Advanced** | Custom CSS, Custom HTML (head) for fonts or analytics, and Custom HTML (footer) for a chat widget. |

<img src="/images/affiliate-portal/portal-login.png" alt="The portal's sign-in page on its own domain with the Northwind Partners header, the login subheading and the brand colors" />

<Warning>
Custom CSS and HTML are applied as is. Broken markup can hide parts of the portal or break it, so open the portal
in a new tab and check it after you save.
</Warning>

## Emails

Under **Transactional emails**:

- **From name** and **Reply-to**: the sender name affiliates see and where their replies go. Portal emails go out
  from a no-reply address, so set a reply-to if affiliates should be able to answer you.
- **Welcome email (account created)**: the email an affiliate gets when you invite them or reset their password. Macros:
  `{{ first_name }}`, `{{ last_name }}`, `{{ email }}`, `{{ portal_name }}`, `{{ login_url }}`,
  `{{ temp_password }}`, `{{ is_reset }}`.
- **Payout status email**: the email an affiliate gets when a payout changes status, and when a payout is held because
  they have no payout details. Macros: `{{ first_name }}`, `{{ amount }}`, `{{ currency }}`, `{{ status }}`,
  `{{ method }}`, `{{ brand_name }}`, `{{ payout_id }}`, `{{ created_at }}`, `{{ note }}`.

Each has a **Subject** and an **HTML body**. Leave a field empty to use the default email.

<img src="/images/affiliate-portal/branding-emails.png" alt="The Transactional emails section: from name, reply-to, and the welcome and payout status subject and body with their macros" />

Every portal email, including the sign-up code and the password reset, carries the portal's logo, primary color
and name. The wording of the sign-up code email, and the approval email from each brand, are not changed here.
