This page is what your affiliates see on the portal's domain. Share the sign-up link with them:
`https://<your portal domain>/affiliates/register`. The sign-in page is at `/affiliates/login`.

## Sign up

The affiliate fills in **Full name**, **Email address**, **Phone** (optional), a **Password** of at least 8 characters
and **Confirm password**, then clicks **Create account**.

<img src="/images/affiliate-portal/portal-register.png" alt="The portal's sign-up form with full name, email, optional phone and password, in the portal's branding" />

## Confirm the email

The portal emails a 6-digit **Verification code**. The affiliate enters it on the next screen. The code is valid for
15 minutes; they can ask for a new one from the same screen.

<img src="/images/affiliate-portal/portal-verify.png" alt="The verification step asking for the 6-digit code sent to the affiliate's email" />

Once the code checks out, the account is created:

- The affiliate is put on every brand on the portal, each with that brand's
  [status after sign-up](/affiliate-portal/brands#status-after-sign-up).
- Your organization's admins get an email about the new sign-up.
- The affiliate is signed in and lands on the dashboard.

## Waiting for approval

On a brand set to **Pending approval**, the affiliate waits until you approve them. They can sign in in the
meantime, and a **Waiting for approval** notice at the top of the dashboard tells them what they can do now:
finish their profile and payout details. Tracking links, creatives and swipes for that brand stay hidden until
they are approved.

You approve them on the affiliate's **Brands** tab; see
[Approve an affiliate](/affiliate-portal/affiliates#approve-an-affiliate). The brand then emails them that they
were approved, and the brand's links and resources appear on the portal.

<img src="/images/affiliate-portal/portal-pending.png" alt="The portal dashboard of an affiliate still waiting for approval, with the Waiting for approval notice at the top" />

## Sign in

Affiliates sign in with their email and password. **Forgot password?** emails them a link to set a new one.

<img src="/images/affiliate-portal/portal-login.png" alt="The portal's sign-in page with email, password and the Forgot password link" />

An affiliate whose account you disabled cannot sign in. If you invited them, they sign in with the temporary
password from the invite email and change it under **Settings → Password**.
