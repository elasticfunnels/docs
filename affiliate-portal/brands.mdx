Affiliates can only promote the brands that are on the portal. Every brand in your organization can be added, and
one affiliate login covers all of them.

## Add brands

Open the portal's **Brands** tab and click **Add**. Tick the brands to add and click **Add N brands**. A brand that
is already on the portal does not appear in the list.

<img src="/images/affiliate-portal/brands-list.png" alt="The Brands tab listing two brands with their Status after sign-up: Pending approval and Active" />

## Status after sign-up

Each brand has a **Status after sign-up**: the status an affiliate gets on that brand when they sign up on the
portal. Open the row's menu, pick **Settings** and choose:

| Status | What the affiliate gets |
|---|---|
| **Pending approval** | They can sign in, but you approve them before they promote the brand. The default for a brand you add. |
| **Active** | They can promote the brand right away. |
| **Disabled** | They are on the brand but cannot use it. |

<img src="/images/affiliate-portal/brand-settings.png" alt="The Brand settings modal with the Status after sign-up select" />

The brand has its own setting too: **Default Status After Register** in the brand's **Settings → Affiliates**.
When both are set, the stricter one applies, in this order: Disabled, then Pending approval, then Active. So a
portal set to Active never auto-approves sign-ups on a brand whose owner wants to approve them.

The status only applies to new sign-ups. Affiliates you invite are Active on every brand you tick in the invite. You
change an existing affiliate's status on their **Brands** tab. See [Affiliates](/affiliate-portal/affiliates).

## Add a brand to every affiliate

Affiliates who signed up before a brand joined the portal are not on that brand. When you add brands to the
portal, or set a brand's **Status after sign-up** to **Active**, a window asks **Add {brand} to your
affiliates?** and says how many of your active affiliates don't have the brand yet.

<img src="/images/affiliate-portal/brand-add-to-affiliates.png" alt="The Add Northwind Sleep to your affiliates? modal with how many active affiliates lack the brand, Not now and Add to affiliates" />

- **Add to affiliates** puts the brand on each of them, with the brand's status after sign-up (Pending approval
  means you still approve each one). Affiliates you removed from the brand are skipped. More than 300 are added
  in the background, and a message tells you so.
- **Not now** closes the window and changes nothing. You can still add the brand to one affiliate on their
  **Brands** tab.

The window only shows when at least one active affiliate lacks the brand, and only to users who can edit
affiliates. Adding several brands at once asks about each brand in turn.

## Remove a brand

Pick **Remove from portal** in the row's menu. Affiliates stop seeing the brand on the portal, and new sign-ups no
longer get it. The brand's internal affiliates stay on the brand, with their history.
