The affiliate portal is a branded site on a domain you own, such as `partners.example.com`. It belongs to your
organization, so one portal can carry several of your brands and one affiliate login can promote all of them.

You run it from **Organization settings → Affiliate portal**. Your affiliates use it on the portal's domain. The
**Setup checklist** on its Overview tab shows what is still missing before affiliates can promote and get paid.

## How it fits together

1. **You create the portal** on a custom domain, add the DNS records it shows and work through the setup
   checklist. See [Set up the portal](/affiliate-portal/set-up).
2. **You put brands on it.** Affiliates can only promote the brands that are on the portal. Each brand sets the
   status a new sign-up gets. See [Brands on the portal](/affiliate-portal/brands).
3. **Affiliates join.** They sign up on the portal and confirm their email, or you invite them. See
   [Affiliates](/affiliate-portal/affiliates) and [Sign-up and sign-in](/affiliate-portal/affiliate-sign-up).
4. **They promote.** Each affiliate builds tracking links to your offer pages and uses the creatives you publish.
   See [Assets](/affiliate-portal/assets) and [Links and resources](/affiliate-portal/affiliate-links).
5. **You pay them.** Commission groups set what they earn on each brand. Payouts are generated per affiliate per
   period, across all their brands. See [Commissions and payouts](/affiliate-portal/payouts).

## Portal affiliates and brand affiliates

A portal affiliate is one login in your organization. For every brand they promote, ElasticFunnels keeps an
internal affiliate on that brand, with its own status and commission group. That is the record you see in the
brand's **Affiliates** list with the type **Internal**, and the one conversions are credited to.

So an affiliate can be approved on one brand and still waiting on another. You can also link a brand's internal
affiliates to the portal from the brand side; see [Internal affiliates](/affiliates/internal-affiliates).
