After signing in, affiliates land on **Home**. The menu on the left holds their reports (**Home**, **UTM report**,
**Clicks**, **Conversions**), what they promote with (**Payouts**, **Tracking links**, **Resources**), their
integrations (**Tracking**, **Postbacks**) and **Settings**.

## Home

<img src="/images/affiliate-portal/portal-dashboard.png" alt="The portal's Home page: commission, EPC, clicks and conversions for the last 30 days, the performance chart, clicks by brand and recent payouts" />

- **Commission**, **EPC** (earnings per click), **Clicks** and **Conversions** for the date range picked at the top.
- **Performance over time**: the same numbers by day.
- **Funnel**: from clicks to conversions.
- **Clicks by brand**, when they promote more than one brand.
- **Recent payouts**, and the **Current balance** they have earned and not been paid yet.

When they promote several brands, the brand picker at the top switches every report between **All brands** and
one brand. The date range they pick carries over to the other reports.

## UTM report

**UTM report** groups their traffic by **Source**, **Medium**, **Campaign**, **Term** or **Content**, with unique
sessions, conversions, conversion rate, sales and commission for each value. Clicking a value drills into it, for
example from a source into its campaigns, and the trail at the top goes back.

<img src="/images/affiliate-portal/portal-utm-report.png" alt="The UTM report broken down by source, with sessions, conversions, CVR, sales and commission per value" />

## Clicks

Every click on their links: date, landing URL, IP address, referrer, the five UTM values and the five sub IDs.
They can search, pick the columns to show and click a row for its details.

<img src="/images/affiliate-portal/portal-clicks.png" alt="The Clicks report with date, URL, referrer, UTM and sub ID columns" />

## Conversions

Every sale credited to them: date, order total, their commission, status, and the UTM values of the click that
brought it. The merchant's order id is not shown to affiliates.

<img src="/images/affiliate-portal/portal-conversions.png" alt="The Conversions report with date, order total, commission and status" />

Clicks and Conversions both have **Export** to download the rows as a CSV file.
