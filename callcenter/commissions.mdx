---
title: 'Commission Tracking'
description: 'Track, approve, and export agent commissions for phone sales'
---

The commission system tracks per-sale commissions for call center agents. Commissions are calculated automatically when an agent places an order through Order Entry.

Go to **Call Center** → **Commissions** in the sidebar.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agent places an order">
    Through the Order Entry page during a phone call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commission is calculated">
    Based on the matching commission rule for that center, disposition, or product (flat amount or percentage of sale).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pending review">
    The commission enters the approval queue with a **Pending** status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Admin approves">
    An admin reviews and approves the commission for payout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Marked as paid">
    After payout, the admin marks the commission as **Paid**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Commission Report

The report shows a filterable table of all commissions.

### Filters

- **Agent**: view a specific agent's commissions
- **Status**: show only pending, approved, paid, or clawed back

### Commission Details

Each entry shows:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Agent** | Who made the sale |
| **Order** | Link to the original transaction |
| **Type** | Base (initial sale) or Upsell (add-on product) |
| **Rate** | The commission rate (flat $ or %) |
| **Amount** | Calculated dollar amount |
| **Order Total** | The total order value |
| **Status** | Pending, Approved, Paid, or Clawed back |

## Approval Workflow

Commissions follow a three-step status flow:

1. **Pending** — Created after a successful sale, awaiting review
2. **Approved** — Admin has verified the commission and approved it for payout
3. **Paid** — Commission has been paid to the agent

### Bulk Actions

- **Approve** — Select multiple pending commissions and approve them at once
- **Mark Paid** — Select approved commissions and mark them as paid in batch

### Nobody approves their own commission

A manager who also takes calls cannot approve or pay their own commissions. When their own rows are part of a selection, EF skips them, approves or pays the rest, and tells them how many were skipped; another manager has to approve those. Only the organization owner can approve their own.

Approvals and payments are recorded in the commission audit log with the person who made them.

### Changing a call's outcome later

Saving a call's outcome again recalculates the commission only while it is still **Pending**. An approved or paid commission keeps its status and amount, and a clawed-back one stays clawed back.

## Refunds and Chargebacks

When a phone sale is refunded, charged back or voided, the agent's commission on it is taken back automatically. It does not matter how the refund reaches EF: issued from Customer Service or the order page, reported by the payment gateway, or sent by the merchant's postback.

| What happened | What happens to the commission |
|---------------|-------------------------------|
| Chargeback | The whole commission is taken back and its status becomes **Clawed back** |
| Void or full refund | The whole commission is taken back and its status becomes **Clawed back** |
| Partial refund | The same share of the commission is taken back (a $25 refund on a $100 order takes back 25%). The status stays as it was until the whole order is refunded |

- The commission report, the leaderboard and the CSV export count commission after reversals, so a refunded sale stops counting as payable.
- If the gateway sends the same refund twice, the commission is reversed once.
- A commission rule's **Clawback on refund** and **Clawback on chargeback** switches and its clawback window still decide whether that rule's commissions are reversed.
- A refund of a phone sale keeps the agent and call center of the original sale, so it shows against that agent.

<Note>
If a commission was already **Paid** when the order is refunded, EF records the amount taken back (and shows the commission as **Clawed back** once the whole order is refunded or charged back), but does not deduct it from the agent's next payout for you. Recover it in your payroll.
</Note>

## Exporting

Click **Export** to download commission data as a CSV file for payroll processing. The export respects your current filters, so you can export by agent or status.

Common payroll workflow:

1. Open **Call Center** → **Commissions**
2. Filter by agent or status if needed
3. Approve or mark paid in bulk
4. Export the final CSV
