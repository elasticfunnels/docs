Refer & Earn is your partner dashboard. Open it from **Refer & Earn** at the bottom of the sidebar, or from **Account settings**. You earn a share of what each account you referred pays us, for as long as it pays.

## Your referrals

The **Overview** tab shows where the people who signed up with your code stand with billing.

<img src="/images/account/refer-and-earn-overview.png" alt="The Your referrals card in Refer and Earn: signups with how many were active this month, on trial with how many have a card on file, expected to pay, paying, and the expected monthly commission" />

| Number | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Signups** | Every account created with your code. The line under it says how many logged in or edited a page in the last 30 days. |
| **On trial** | Accounts still in their free trial. The line under it says how many have saved a card. |
| **Expected to pay** | On trial on a paid plan, with a card saved, and the trial ends within 14 days. These are the ones most likely to start paying soon. |
| **Paying** | On a paid plan and charged at least once. An account on the free Hobby plan never counts as paying. The line under it counts accounts that canceled or moved to the free plan. |
| **Expected commission** | What your share of the paying and expected-to-pay accounts comes to each month, at your commission rate. It is an estimate: an expected account only earns you money once it is charged. |

**Your earnings**, below, is money already earned: pending, cleared and paid out.

## Referred users

The **Referred Users** tab lists every signup, one row each. Use the tabs above the list to show only the accounts that are paying, expected to pay, on trial, churned or only signed up.

<img src="/images/account/refer-and-earn-referrals.png" alt="The Referred Users tab in Refer and Earn: stage tabs with counts and an anonymised row per signup showing the code used, stage, plan, trial end, card on file and paying" />

Each row shows the code used, the stage, the plan, when the trial ends, whether a card is saved, whether the account is paying, the monthly commission it is worth to you, and when your first payment from it is due.

Rows do not show who the person is. Names and email addresses stay hidden unless the ElasticFunnels team has turned them on for your partnership; ask your partner manager if you need to follow up with the people you referred.

<Note>
Some partner codes are set up to show totals only. If the tab says the list is not shared for your code, the numbers on **Overview** still include every signup.
</Note>
