title: 'Audience Destinations'
description: 'Send the people who enter or leave an audience to Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp, a signed webhook or an automation'
---

A destination keeps another tool in step with one of your audiences. When someone enters the audience, they are
added to a list or tag, sent to your webhook, or run through an automation. When they leave, they can be taken off
again or sent as a second event. Syncs run after each audience refresh, in batches.

Destinations work on **customer audiences** (people identified by email). An audience that identifies anonymous
visitors cannot be sent anywhere, and a live audience is matched on the page as people browse, so it has no members
to send.

## Where to find it

Open **Reports → Audiences**, pick an audience, and scroll to **Destinations** on its **Overview** tab. Each
destination shows where it sends, when it sends, its holdout and cooldown, the last sync, and its counts:

| Count | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **In audience** | People in the audience now |
| **Sent** | People this destination has sent |
| **Held out** | People in the holdout group, recorded and never sent |
| **No consent** | People your consent settings refused |
| **Failed** | Sends that still failed after 5 attempts |
| **Pending** | People waiting for a later refresh (over the per-run cap, paused integration, or a retry) |

Each row has **Edit**, **Sync now**, **Pause** / **Resume** and **Delete**. Deleting a destination leaves
people already sent where they are. An audience can have up to 20 destinations.

## Add a destination

Click **Add destination** and choose where to send:

| Destination | What happens when someone enters | When someone leaves |
|---|---|---|
| **Klaviyo list** | Added to the list | Removed from the list |
| **ActiveCampaign tag** | Tagged | Tag removed |
| **ActiveCampaign list** | Subscribed to the list (people who left the checkout opt-in unticked are skipped) | Unsubscribed from the list |
| **Mailchimp audience** | Added to the audience, with an optional tag | The tag is removed (leaving never unsubscribes anyone, so a tag is needed to take people off) |
| **Webhook** | `audience.entered` is POSTed to your URL | `audience.left` is POSTed to your URL |
| **Automation** | An automation that starts with **On Entered Audience** runs once per person | An automation that starts with **On Left Audience** runs once per person |

The picker lists the Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign and Mailchimp integrations installed on the brand. A tool you have not
connected is named under the picker with an **Open Integrations** link. An automation is offered when its trigger is
set to this audience; automations run for up to 500 people per refresh, and the rest follow at the next one.

Then set:

- **When**: **They enter**, **They leave**, or both.
- **Only when they left because**: limit leave events to **Bought**, **Refunded or charged back**, **Aged out** or
  **Audience edited**. Leave all unticked for every reason.
- **Holdout**: a share of people (0 to 50%) that is never sent. Each person's group stays the same, so you can
  compare the held-out people with the rest and see the real lift.
- **Cooldown**: someone who re-enters within this many days (0 to 30, default 7) is not sent again.
- **Subscribe people who opted in** (Klaviyo and Mailchimp): subscribes everyone except people who left the email
  opt-in unticked at checkout. Without it, people are added without a marketing subscription.
- **Also sync the people already in this audience**: off by default, so only people who enter from now on are
  sent.

When **Save** is disabled, the line next to it says what is missing, for example "Enter the webhook URL" or
"The URL must start with https://".

### Paused integrations

A paused integration still appears in the picker, marked **(paused)**, and its destinations show an
**Integration paused** badge. You can save a destination on it, but nothing is sent while it is paused: people
wait as **Pending** and are sent once you resume the integration under **Integrations**.

## Edit a destination

Click **Edit** on a destination to change it after saving: the list or tag, the webhook URL and fields, when it
sends, the leave reasons, holdout, cooldown and subscribe option. The destination itself (Klaviyo, a webhook,
an automation) and an ActiveCampaign tag/list choice cannot be changed; to send somewhere else, add another
destination.

## Who is never sent

Every person who enters goes through the same checks, whatever the destination:

1. **Consent**: your brand's consent settings. Email destinations follow the same rule as email campaigns:
   everyone is sent except people who left the checkout opt-in box unticked. Refusals are counted under
   **No consent**.
2. **Email suppression list**: people on the brand's suppression list (unsubscribed, bounced or complained) are
   never added to a list, sent to a webhook, or run through an automation. They are counted, not sent.
3. **No email**: a person with no known email address is skipped.

Removals (people leaving) do not need consent.

## Webhooks

A webhook destination POSTs JSON to your URL. The URL must use HTTPS and point to a public address.

### Signing secret

When you save a new webhook, its signing secret is shown once. Copy it then; it is not shown again.

To change it, open **Edit**, tick **Replace the signing secret** and save. The old secret stops working at once,
and the new one is shown once. Update your receiver right after.

### Headers

| Header | Value |
|---|---|
| `X-EF-Event` | `audience.entered` or `audience.left` |
| `X-EF-Delivery` | The body's `delivery_id` |
| `X-EF-Timestamp` | Unix time in seconds when the request was signed |
| `X-EF-Signature` | `sha256=` followed by the hex HMAC-SHA256 of `<timestamp>.<raw body>`, keyed with the signing secret |

Verify the signature on the raw body before you parse it, and reject a timestamp more than 5 minutes old:

```js
import crypto from 'node:crypto';

function verify(rawBody, headers, secret) {
  const ts = headers['x-ef-timestamp'];
  if (Math.abs(Date.now() / 1000 - Number(ts)) > 300) return false;
  const expected = 'sha256=' + crypto.createHmac('sha256', secret).update(`${ts}.${rawBody}`).digest('hex');
  const given = String(headers['x-ef-signature'] || '');
  return given.length === expected.length && crypto.timingSafeEqual(Buffer.from(given), Buffer.from(expected));
}
```

### Body

Each request carries up to 500 people. Larger batches are split into several requests.

```json
{
  "event": "audience.entered",
  "audience": { "id": 8, "name": "Took the order bump" },
  "activation_id": 41,
  "run_id": 412,
  "delivery_id": "3f1c9a7e-52b4-5d0e-9a61-0c4e2b7d8f13",
  "sent_at": "2026-10-18T10:15:02Z",
  "people": [
    {
      "email_hash": "9b74c9897bac770ffc029102a200c5de",
      "email": "jane@example.com",
      "first_name": "Jane",
      "last_name": "Doe",
      "phone": "+15555550123",
      "reason": ""
    }
  ]
}
```

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `event` | `audience.entered` or `audience.left` |
| `audience` | The audience's id and name |
| `activation_id` | The destination's id |
| `run_id` | The audience refresh that produced this change |
| `delivery_id` | The id of this POST (see [Duplicates and retries](#duplicates-and-retries)) |
| `sent_at` | When the request was sent, UTC |
| `people[].email_hash` | A stable id for the person, present in every shape |
| `people[].reason` | Why they left: `bought`, `refunded`, `aged_out` or `definition_changed`. Empty when they enter |

### Fields for each person

Under **Fields for each person** you choose the shape of each entry in `people`:

- **Standard fields** (the default): `email_hash`, `email`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `phone` and `reason`, as in
  the example above.
- **Pick columns, as in the CSV export**: `email_hash` and `reason`, plus exactly the columns you tick. The list is
  the same one the audience's CSV export offers, including the audience's own computed columns (for example which
  order bump each buyer took).

With picked columns, a person looks like this:

```json
{
  "email_hash": "9b74c9897bac770ffc029102a200c5de",
  "reason": "",
  "email": "jane@example.com",
  "first_name": "Jane",
  "orders": 2,
  "total_revenue": 232,
  "last_purchase_at": "2026-10-17 09:27:00",
  "bump_product": ["Sleep Gummies, add a 30-day jar"]
}
```

A column that has no value for a person is sent as `null`. If you later edit the audience so a picked column no
longer exists, saving the destination asks you to pick the columns again.

### Duplicates and retries

A `429`, a `5xx` or a dropped connection is retried, and a send that still fails is tried again at later refreshes,
up to 5 attempts in all. When a batch is retried, every request in it is sent again, including ones your receiver
already accepted.

`delivery_id` is built from the destination, the refresh, the event and the people in the request, so a retried
request carries the same `delivery_id` (also in `X-EF-Delivery`) and any other request gets a new one. To ignore
duplicates:

1. Store each `delivery_id` you have processed.
2. When a request arrives with a `delivery_id` you already have, answer `200` and do nothing else.
3. Answer `2xx` only after you have stored the people. Any other status counts as a failed send.

If your receiver also writes per person, key it on `email_hash` plus `event`, so the same person entering twice in
a row is still one record.
