Every email template opens in one of two editors. The **Visual Builder** is a drag-and-drop canvas
made for email: its blocks are built from tables, so they look the same in Gmail, Outlook and Apple
Mail. The **Code Editor** is a plain HTML editor for people who would rather write the markup. You
choose one when you [create the template](/emails/overview#create-a-template), and both are in
each row's menu in the template list (**Settings → Apps → Emails**).

Click a template's name in the list to open it. The editor opens in a new browser tab, so the list
stays where you left it.

## The parts of the builder

- **The canvas** in the middle is the email itself, shown on the email's own background color. Double-click
  any text to type in it, click an image or a button to select it.
- **The left bar** holds the blocks panel, layers, your files, fonts, brand colors and the
  **Email Variables** panel.
- **The style panel** on the right changes the selected element: colors, spacing, font size,
  alignment, the link of a button.
- **The top bar** has the desktop, tablet and mobile view buttons, undo and redo, and **Save**.
  The gear button opens the template settings: name, subject, preview text, sender and
  [versions](/emails/overview#versions).
- **The (i) button** in the left bar opens **About this email**. For a template it shows the subject,
  preview text, sender, whether the template is sending and why, what uses it and the saved version,
  with a button to edit the subject and sender. For a campaign email it shows the campaign, its
  status, subject, preview text and the sender the send will use, with a button that saves and
  takes you to the campaign settings.

## Add a section

A small **+** marker sits between the sections of the email, and above the first and below the
last. Move the pointer near one and it opens into an **Add section here** line.

<Steps>
<Step title="Click Add section here">
Pick the gap where the new section should go and click the line (or the **+**).
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a block">
A picker opens with **Text**, **Image**, **Button**, **Two columns**, **Divider**, **Spacer**,
**Social links**, **Logo**, **Footer** and **Custom HTML**. Click one and it is inserted at that
spot.
</Step>
<Step title="Edit it in place">
The new section is selected. Type over the sample text, change the image, set the button link in
the style panel.
</Step>
</Steps>

The markers only exist in the editor. They are never saved into the email and never reach an inbox.

You can also drag blocks from the blocks panel onto the canvas. The **Email elements** group holds
the same blocks as the picker.

## The email blocks

| Block | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Text** | A heading and a paragraph. |
| **Image** | A full-width image. Replace it with one from your files or a URL. |
| **Button** | A link styled as a button, built from a table so it shows in every mail client. |
| **Two columns** | Two blocks of text side by side. |
| **Divider** | A line between two parts of the email. |
| **Spacer** | Empty space between sections. |
| **Social links** | Links to your social profiles. Change each link to your own pages. |
| **Logo** | Your logo, centered. |
| **Footer** | Your brand name, your postal address and the unsubscribe link. Use it in campaigns. |
| **Custom HTML** | Markup you paste yourself. See [Your own HTML](#your-own-html). |

Blocks that only work on web pages, such as frontend template engine blocks, Include Component,
Extends Layout, Block and Backend Script, are not offered in the email builder. The backend
**If/Else**, **Foreach** and **Set** blocks are there, for [conditional content](#conditional-content).

## Your own HTML

There are two ways to bring in markup of your own:

- **Custom HTML block.** Pick **Custom HTML** from an **Add section here** line, or drag the
  **HTML Block** onto the canvas, then click it and paste your code. The canvas shows the result
  rendered, as it will look in the inbox. Scripts do not run in email, in the builder or in any
  mail client.
- **Edit HTML on any element.** Right-click an element and choose **Edit HTML**, or select it and
  press `Ctrl+E` (`Cmd+E` on a Mac). Change the markup and the element is redrawn with your
  change.

For a whole email written by hand, use the Code Editor instead: create the template with **Code
Editor** as its editor, or open an existing template with **Code** from its row menu. It has syntax
highlighting and a **Format code** button, and `Ctrl+S` / `Cmd+S` saves.

## Variables

Variables put the customer's details into the email when it is sent: `{{ purchase.customer_first_name }}`
becomes "Jane". Which variables a template can use depends on its type (the **Variable scope** in the
template settings): a Purchase template has the order, a Renewal reminder has the subscription.

Click the **Email Variables** button in the left bar to see the list for this template. Click a
variable to copy it, then paste it into the text. Pin the panel to keep it open while you write.
If the panel says no variables are available, set a **Variable scope** in the template settings
first.

Variables work in the subject and preview text too. The full list is on
[Template Variables](/emails/template-variables).

## Conditional content

Show a section only when the data is there, with `@if`:

```html
@if(purchase.tracking_url)
<a href="{{ purchase.tracking_url }}">Track your package</a>
@endif
```

Repeat a block for each product in the order with `@each`:

```html
@each(product in products)
<p>{{ product.name }}: {{ product.total }}</p>
@endeach
```

Put these in a Custom HTML block, or use the **If/Else** and **Foreach** blocks from the blocks
panel.

## Check it before it goes out

- **Mobile.** Use the desktop, tablet and mobile buttons in the top bar to see the email at each
  width. Most email is read on a phone.
- **A real inbox.** Save, go back to the template list (**Settings → Apps → Emails**), open the template's row menu and click
  **Send test**. Type the address to send it to. The test goes out through your brand SMTP, or
  through one of your email integrations when the brand has no SMTP, so it also shows whether your
  sender works.
- **What was sent.** Every send, tests included, is listed in **Settings → Logs → Email Logs**,
  with the template version it used. **Open send logs** in the info panel opens it filtered to this
  template.

## Save

Click **Save** in the top bar. Saving changes to the subject, sender or content creates a new
version of the template, and the old one stays readable under **Versions**. See
[Versions](/emails/overview#versions).

Changes reach customers from the next email that is sent. Emails already sent are not changed.

## Emails that belong to a campaign

When [campaigns](/emails/overview#transactional-emails-and-campaigns) are on for your brand, each
campaign has its own email, edited in the same builder.

- **A new campaign starts on a default layout**: your brand name, a headline, a short text, a
  button and a footer with your postal address and the unsubscribe link. Edit it in place instead
  of starting from an empty page.
- On the campaign's **Content** step, **Edit design** opens the email in the Visual Builder and
  **Edit HTML** opens it in the Code Editor. **Replace content** starts over from **A template**,
  **An earlier campaign** or **The default layout**. Replacing changes only this campaign; your
  templates stay as they are.
- In the builder, **Continue to settings** next to **Save** saves the email and takes you to the
  campaign's **Settings** step, where you set the sender, the subject and your postal address.
  **Back to campaign** returns to the Content step.
- The subject field in **Settings** has buttons to insert **First name**, **Full name** and
  **Brand name**.
- Keep the unsubscribe link and your postal address in the email. If either is missing when the
  campaign is sent, ElasticFunnels adds a short footer with them.
- On the **Review and send** step, **Send test** sends the email to you first, with your name in
  place of the recipient's.
