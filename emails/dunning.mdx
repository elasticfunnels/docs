## What is dunning?
{/* SCREENSHOT: What is dunning? · email editor or template settings · desktop */}

Dunning is the process of retrying failed subscription payments and communicating with customers about payment issues. ElasticFunnels automates this with configurable retry schedules and escalating email notifications.

## Retry schedule
{/* SCREENSHOT: Retry schedule · email editor or template settings · desktop */}

When a renewal charge fails, the subscription moves to **Past Due** and ElasticFunnels retries the charge on a schedule. Each failed attempt sends the **Renewal Failed** email. The default schedule is `1, 3, 5, 7, 10, 14` with **Max Retries** set to 6:

| Charge attempt | When | If it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Renewal | On the next charge date | Renewal Failed email, retry in 1 day |
| Retry 1 | 1 day after the renewal failed | Renewal Failed email, retry in 3 days |
| Retry 2 | 3 days after retry 1 | Renewal Failed email, retry in 5 days |
| Retry 3 | 5 days after retry 2 | Renewal Failed email, retry in 7 days |
| Retry 4 | 7 days after retry 3 | Renewal Failed email, retry in 10 days |
| Retry 5 | 10 days after retry 4 | Sixth failure: the subscription is canceled |

Each number in the schedule is the wait after the previous failed attempt, so the default schedule cancels about 26 days after the first failed renewal. **Max Retries** counts every failed attempt, the renewal included. When the count reaches it, the subscription is canceled with the reason `dunning_exhausted` and the [Subscription Canceled](/emails/subscription-emails#subscription-canceled) email is sent. A successful retry puts the subscription back to **Active**, and the next charge date becomes one billing cycle after the day the retry succeeded.

### Retry timing follows the decline

On the default schedule, ElasticFunnels adjusts the waits to the reason the card was declined:

| Decline | Waits between attempts (days) |
|---|---|
| Insufficient funds | 2, 5, 10, 15, 21, 28 |
| Card unusable (stolen, lost, expired, closed, fraud) | 3, 7, 14, 21, 28, 35 |
| Temporary issuer decline, or unknown | 1, 3, 5, 7, 10, 14 |

American Express and Discover cards get one extra day on every wait. The number of attempts never changes, only the timing. If you set your own schedule on the merchant, ElasticFunnels uses it as entered (plus the card-brand day).

### Other stored cards first

When an NMI renewal is declined, ElasticFunnels immediately tries the customer's other cards stored in the brand; for Stripe, the other cards on the Stripe customer. A card that succeeds becomes the subscription's card, and the retry schedule never starts.

## Configuring the retry schedule
{/* SCREENSHOT: Settings → Merchants → merchant → Email tab → Dunning Configuration · desktop */}

The schedule is set per merchant. Go to **Settings** → **Merchants**, open the merchant, and switch to the **Email** tab. In **Dunning Configuration** you can change:

- **Max Retries**: failed attempts before the subscription is canceled (default 6)
- **Retry Schedule (days)**: the wait before each retry (default `1, 3, 5, 7, 10, 14`)
- **Callcenter Escalation After Retry #**: send the customer to your call center after this many failures (0 turns it off)
- **Send to Callcenter on Cancel**: also send dunning cancellations to your call center

Dunning Configuration shows on NMI and Stripe merchants. PayPal and Authorize.net subscriptions renew and retry too, on the default schedule.

An active organization or brand schedule under **Call Center** → **Billing** → **Dunning** overrides the merchant's schedule. Its offsets are in hours and are rounded up to whole days.

## Escalating email urgency
{/* SCREENSHOT: Escalating email urgency · email editor or template settings · desktop */}

The `renewal-failed` template uses the `subscription.retry_count` variable to escalate urgency:

```html
@if(subscription.retry_count)
  @if(subscription.retry_count gte 5)
    <!-- Red: Final warning -->
    <p>Your subscription will be canceled unless you update your payment.</p>
  @elseif(subscription.retry_count gte 3)
    <!-- Orange: Getting serious -->
    <p>We've tried multiple times. Please update your card.</p>
  @else
    <!-- Yellow: First notice -->
    <p>We had trouble processing your payment. We'll try again soon.</p>
  @endif
@endif
```

`retry_count` counts the failed renewal as 1. From the 5th failed attempt the email shows a red **Final Notice** banner, so with the default Max Retries of 6 the email after attempt 5 is the final notice. If attempt 6 fails, the Subscription Canceled email goes out instead.

<Warning>
There is no page yet where a customer can enter a new card. The default template's **Update Payment Method** button opens your `/members` page, which has no card form, so edit the button to point to your support email or phone.
</Warning>

## Dunning final notice
{/* SCREENSHOT: Dunning final notice · email editor or template settings · desktop */}

The `dunning-final` template is meant as a last notice before cancellation, but no event sends it
yet, and new brands do not get it. Brands that already have it can edit it. It features:

- A red "Final Notice" banner
- Bold "Pending Cancellation" status
- Support contact information (phone and email from Brand Settings)
- An "Update Payment Method" CTA

## Callcenter escalation
{/* SCREENSHOT: Callcenter escalation · email editor or template settings · desktop */}

If you have the Callcenter module enabled, failed payments can be escalated to your support team automatically. When a renewal has failed the number of times set in **Callcenter Escalation After Retry #**, the event is sent to your call center integrations so an agent can call the customer. The count includes the failed renewal, so the default of 3 escalates when retry 2 fails. With the in-house call center connected (the Organization Callcenter integration), agents get the lead one at a time in the Agent Workstation.

Set it on the merchant's **Email** tab, in **Dunning Configuration** (see above). Each [call center integration](/integrations/callcenters) decides which events it receives.

## Support contact in emails
{/* SCREENSHOT: Support contact in emails · email editor or template settings · desktop */}

The `brand.support_phone` and `brand.support_email` variables (from Brand Settings > General) are shown in dunning emails to give customers a way to resolve payment issues. Make sure these are configured before enabling dunning.
