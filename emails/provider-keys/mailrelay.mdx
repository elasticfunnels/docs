The Mailrelay integration sends over SMTP. It asks for **SMTP Host**, **SMTP Port**, **Use SSL/TLS**,
**SMTP Username** and **SMTP Password**, plus your **Default From Email**, **Default From Name** and
**Default Reply-To Email**. The password is stored encrypted and never shown again.

## SMTP settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open SMTP settings">
    Log in to Mailrelay and go to **Settings** → **SMTP settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the values">
    Copy the server, port, user name and password shown there into **SMTP Host**, **SMTP Port**,
    **SMTP Username** and **SMTP Password**. Turn on **Use SSL/TLS** when the port Mailrelay lists uses it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the sender">
    Use a **Default From Email** on a domain you have verified as a sender in Mailrelay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Leave **Disable TLS validation** off unless Mailrelay support tells you otherwise.

Mailrelay's own guide: [SMTP settings](https://help.mailrelay.com/en/support/solutions/articles/103000160464-smtp-settings).

The same form works for any SMTP server: enter that server's host, port and login.
