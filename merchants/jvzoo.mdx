This guide connects JVZoo as a merchant, for a vendor selling their own product through JVZoo's checkout. Your pages run on ElasticFunnels; JVZoo takes the payment, runs the upsell sequence and reports each order back over JVZIPN.

<Note>
**This page is for vending your own products on JVZoo.** Promoting other sellers' JVZoo products is the separate [JVZoo Affiliate S2S integration](/integrations/jvzoo#jvzoo-affiliate-s2s).

The two use different tracking parameters: `vtid` carries your click code on your own buy links, `tid` is the affiliate's SubID. ElasticFunnels never writes to `tid` on a vendor buy link, so an affiliate's own sub-tracking passes through untouched.
</Note>

<Tip>
Prefer a walkthrough with a screenshot for each step? See the Academy tutorial [Sell on JVZoo with ElasticFunnels](https://elasticfunnels.io/academy/sell-on-jvzoo).
</Tip>

## How JVZoo differs from other merchants

JVZoo is a **hosted checkout**. Your funnel owns the sales pages; JVZoo owns the cart, the payment and the upsell sequence.

- There is **no checkout API**. Buyers leave your page for JVZoo's checkout through a buy link.
- **Upsells are charged by JVZoo.** On your upsell pages, `[UPSELL=...]` tags build JVZoo buy links for the upsell products; ElasticFunnels never charges a JVZoo buyer itself.
- The thank-you page is set **per product in JVZoo**, not passed on the buy link.

## The three numbers of a JVZoo buy link

Everything ElasticFunnels needs from JVZoo is in the buy link JVZoo generates for your product (Sellers, Seller's Dashboard, **Actions** next to the product, **Buy Buttons**, then copy the button's link):

```
https://www.jvzoo.com/b/120456/451020/99
                        ^       ^      ^
                        funnel  product button
```

| Part | What it is | Where it goes in ElasticFunnels |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `120456` | Your JVZoo funnel ID. JVZIPN reports it as `funnel_id`. | **JVZoo Funnel ID** on the merchant |
| `451020` | The product ID. Each product in the funnel has its own. | **jvzoo Product ID** on the product's Merchants tab |
| `99` | The button ID, the third number | **JVZoo Button ID** on the funnel's domain row |

The numbers above are examples. The merchant's **JVZoo Funnel ID** takes the **first** number and the funnel domain row's **JVZoo Button ID** takes the **third**. ElasticFunnels builds every buy link as `/b/<JVZoo Funnel ID>/<product ID>/<JVZoo Button ID>`, so with both filled in the links match the ones JVZoo gives you.

<Warning>
Your JVZoo **vendor ID** (the account number on your JVZoo profile) is a different number and goes nowhere in this setup. Typed into the merchant's JVZoo Funnel ID, it produces buy links JVZoo does not recognise.
</Warning>

If the product sits in a JVZoo funnel with upsells, copy the button from that funnel so the first number is the funnel's ID.

## Step 1: Create the JVZoo merchant

1. Go to **Settings** → **Merchants** and click **Create Merchant**
2. Pick **JVZoo** as the **Gateway**, type a **Name**, and click **Create & Configure**

<img src="/images/merchants/jvzoo-create.png" alt="The Create a new merchant dialog with JVZoo picked as the Gateway, the name Northwind JVZoo and the Create & Configure button" />

## Step 2: Enter the funnel ID and the JVZIPN secret key

On the merchant's settings:

1. **JVZoo Funnel ID**: the first number of your buy link (`120456` above)
2. **JVZIPN Secret Key**, under **JVZIPN Configuration**: the secret key from your JVZoo account. Third-party JVZIPN guides place it on JVZoo's **My Account** page, in the **JVZIPN Secret Key** field; you choose the key there and paste the same value here.
3. Click **Save**

<img src="/images/merchants/jvzoo-settings.png" alt="A JVZoo merchant's settings: JVZoo Funnel ID, the Accept postbacks without verifying the key toggle and the JVZIPN Secret Key field" />

**Accept postbacks without verifying the key** is on by default. While it is on, JVZIPNs are recorded even with no key or a key that does not match, so no order is lost while you set things up. Once the key is in and sales arrive, turn it off: from then on a JVZIPN without a valid `cverify` signature is refused (`400 missing secret` with no key, `401 invalid signature` with a wrong one) and nothing is recorded for it.

## Step 3: Paste the JVZIPN URL into JVZoo

1. In **Settings** → **Merchants**, click **Postback Setup** on the JVZoo merchant's row (or **Copy Postback URL** in its row menu). Its steps are listed on the left: **Postback URL**, **Set up in JVZoo** and **Test**.
2. Copy **Your Postback URL**. It has the form `https://app.elasticfunnels.io/postback/vendor/<code>/jvzoo`, where `<code>` belongs to this merchant.
3. In JVZoo, edit the product and paste it as the product's **JVZIPN URL**. Third-party guides place this under **External Program Integration** in the product editor. Repeat for every product in the funnel, upsells included.

<img src="/images/merchants/jvzoo-postback-setup-steps.png" alt="The Postback Setup dialog for a JVZoo merchant on Set up in JVZoo: the steps list, the postback URL with Copy and the four steps in JVZoo" />

The merchant shows **Needs Setup** until its first JVZIPN is accepted; it then switches to **Verified**. Sales, rebills, refunds, chargebacks, subscription cancels and reactivations, and abandoned orders all arrive at this one URL.

JVZoo has no button that sends a test notification. Place a test order, or wait for the first real sale: the first JVZIPN that reaches ElasticFunnels marks the merchant verified. The **Test** step of Postback Setup shows the status while it is open.

<Note>
Use the merchant's URL, not the **JVZoo Vendor IPN** integration's. The merchant matches each order to your products, links refunds and chargebacks to the original sale, removes duplicate notifications when **Deduplicate Orders** is on, and records the affiliate. The JVZoo Vendor IPN integration under Integrations is a separate, simpler receiver that stores the product name only. See [JVZoo Vendor IPN](/integrations/jvzoo#jvzoo-vendor-ipn).
</Note>

## Step 4: Map each JVZoo product ID

1. Go to **Settings** → **Products** and open the product
2. On the **Merchants** tab, fill **jvzoo (&lt;funnel ID&gt;) Product ID** with the middle number of its buy link (`451020` above)
3. Save, then repeat for every product in the funnel, upsells included

<img src="/images/merchants/jvzoo-product-id.png" alt="A product's Merchants tab with the jvzoo Product ID field set to 451020" />

The same ID builds the buy link and matches incoming JVZIPNs to the product. With no mapping, the product's own **Code** is used, which only works if the code is the JVZoo number. A JVZIPN for a JVZoo product that matches nothing raises a **Create Missing Product** action and the order is saved without that product line.

## Step 5: Assign the merchant to the domain

1. Go to **Settings** → **Domains**, open the domain's menu and click **Edit**
2. Pick your JVZoo merchant under **Merchant** and **Save**

<img src="/images/merchants/jvzoo-domain.png" alt="The Edit domain dialog with the Northwind JVZoo merchant picked and the Buy Link Parameters tab" />

Every `[BUY=...]` and `[UPSELL=...]` tag on a page of this domain now builds a JVZoo buy link.

## Step 6: Enter the button ID on the funnel's domain row

1. Go to **Funnels**, open the funnel's menu and click **Edit**
2. Under **Domains**, on the row of your JVZoo domain, type the **third** number of the buy link (`99` above) into **JVZoo Button ID**
3. Save

<img src="/images/merchants/jvzoo-funnel-row.png" alt="A funnel's domain row with the JVZoo Button ID field set to 99" />

The value lives on the domain row, so one funnel served on two domains can use two different JVZoo buttons. The field is required on a JVZoo domain when you save the funnel. On an older funnel saved without it, buy links have two numbers and a trailing slash (`/b/120456/451020/`); add the button ID so they match JVZoo's own links.

## Buy links

Use the standard tag on your sales page:

```html
<a href="[BUY=sleep-gummies-1]">Buy Now</a>
```

With the merchant, the product mapping and the domain row set, it becomes:

```
https://www.jvzoo.com/b/120456/451020/99?vtid=<click_code>&aff=<affiliate_id>&aid=<affiliate_id>
```

| Part | Source |
| --- | --- |
| `120456` | Merchant **JVZoo Funnel ID** |
| `451020` | The product's **jvzoo Product ID** |
| `99` | **JVZoo Button ID** on the funnel's domain row |
| `vtid` | The visitor's ElasticFunnels click code, read back from the JVZIPN to attribute the sale |
| `aff`, `aid` | The JVZoo affiliate ID, when the visit came from an affiliate. `aid` is the name JVZoo's own link script uses; both carry the same value |

`subid`, `subid2` and `subid3` on the page URL are passed through, and the domain's **Buy Link Parameters** are added to every link.

### Upsell pages

Upsell and downsell pages use `[UPSELL=code]` and `[DOWNSELL=code]`. On a JVZoo domain they build the JVZoo buy link for that product, with the same click code, so the upsell sale lands on the same visitor. Map every upsell product's JVZoo ID (step 4) and put the products in the same order JVZoo's funnel expects. If JVZoo gives you a script for your sales or upsell pages, paste it in the page's **Settings** → **Tracking** → **Custom Header Code**.

### Direct purchase links

A hardcoded JVZoo link works too. **It must carry `vtid`**, or the sale arrives with no click attribution. Use the `{click_id}` token and ElasticFunnels substitutes the visitor's click code when the page renders:

```html
<a href="https://www.jvzoo.com/b/120456/451024/99?vtid={click_id}">Buy Now</a>
```

In a backend script, use the template-engine syntax instead:

```
https://www.jvzoo.com/b/120456/451024/99?vtid={{ click_id }}
```

`{click_id}` and `[click_id]` (and their URL-encoded forms) are the legacy page-HTML substitutions and are still supported; `{{ click_id }}` is the current template-engine syntax.

<Warning>
The legacy `{click_id}` form is **case-sensitive**. `{CLICK_ID}` is not substituted and ships the literal text to JVZoo, which then posts back a tracking ID that matches no click.
</Warning>

Prefer `[BUY=...]` tags where you can: they pick up the merchant, the product mapping, the domain row and the affiliate ID automatically.

## Thank-you page

JVZoo sends the buyer to the thank-you page set on each product in JVZoo. Set it to the thank-you page on your ElasticFunnels domain (for example `https://example.com/thank-you`). The order itself is recorded from the JVZIPN, so the thank-you page does not need to carry any order data. When JVZoo adds `transaction_id`, `receipt` or `cbreceipt` to the URL, the page knows the visitor is a customer and can show the order number.

## Tracking pixel

The quickest way: on the JVZoo merchant's settings, turn on **Create Tracking Pixels** and save. ElasticFunnels adds the JVZoo pixel as a tracking script on all pages, in the footer, once per merchant.

To add it by hand instead:

1. Go to **Pages** → **Tracking Scripts** and click **Create Tracking Script**
2. Under **Template**, pick **JVZoo**
3. Set **Placement** to **Footer** and save

<img src="/images/merchants/jvzoo-pixel.png" alt="A new tracking script with the JVZoo template, Footer placement and the i.jvzoo.com image tag" />

```html
<img src='https://i.jvzoo.com/{jvz_acc_id}/{product_code_first}/{jvz_funnel_id}' border="0" width="1" height="1" />
```

Nothing to fill in. The tokens resolve per page:

| Token | Resolves to |
| --- | --- |
| `{jvz_acc_id}` | The merchant's **JVZoo Funnel ID** |
| `{product_code_first}` | The JVZoo ID of the first buy button on the page |
| `{jvz_funnel_id}` | **JVZoo Button ID** on the funnel's domain row |
| `{product_codes_comma}` | Every product code on the page, comma-separated |

Use **Footer**: an image tag in the head ends the head early in the browser, and any head script after it is moved into the body.

## Affiliate attribution

JVZoo only tells you which affiliate sent a visitor if you turn it on, **per product**:

1. In the JVZoo product editor, find the **sales page URL** field
2. Set **Pass Affiliate ID to Sales Page** to **Yes**
3. Repeat for every product in the funnel

JVZoo then adds `aid=<affiliate_id>` when it hands the visitor to your page. ElasticFunnels keeps it for the session and puts it on the buy link as `aff` and `aid`, and the JVZIPN brings the affiliate and commission back with the sale.

<Warning>
With this setting off, affiliate traffic arrives with no affiliate ID and those sales cannot be attributed afterwards.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Buy links go to the wrong JVZoo page">
    Compare the link on your page with the one JVZoo gives you under Buy Buttons. The merchant's **JVZoo Funnel ID** must be the first number (not your vendor ID), the product's **jvzoo Product ID** the middle one, and the domain row's **JVZoo Button ID** the third one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Upsells never run after the front-end sale">
    Check that the button you copied came from the JVZoo funnel, so the first number is the funnel's ID, and that every upsell product has its JVZoo ID mapped.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The merchant still says Needs Setup">
    No JVZIPN has been accepted yet. Check that the JVZIPN URL in JVZoo is the merchant's URL from Postback Setup, on every product. With **Accept postbacks without verifying the key** off, a missing or wrong secret key also refuses every JVZIPN.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sales arrive without a product">
    The JVZoo product ID is not mapped on any product. Fill **jvzoo Product ID** on the product's Merchants tab, or accept the **Create Missing Product** action.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sales recorded with no click attribution">
    The JVZIPN arrived without a tracking ID matching an ElasticFunnels click code. Either the link was hardcoded without `?vtid={click_id}`, or the JVZIPN went to another URL. Every JVZIPN received is listed under **Settings** → **Logs** → **Merchant Postbacks**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Affiliate sales recorded with no affiliate">
    **Pass Affiliate ID to Sales Page** is off for that product in JVZoo, so no `aid` reached your page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pixel renders with an empty product ID">
    `{product_code_first}` reads the buy tags on the page. A page with no `[BUY=...]` or `[UPSELL=...]` tag has no product to report.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
