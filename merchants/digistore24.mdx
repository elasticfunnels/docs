This guide explains how to set up Digistore24 as a merchant. The overall flow matches the generic merchant setup; use this page for platform-specific notes.

## Step 1: Create the Digistore24 merchant

<Note>
Creating a Digistore24 merchant opens a guided setup: account details, product IDs, the postback URL and a live test, one step at a time. You can stop and come back: **Continue setup** on the merchant's row resumes it. See [Guided setup](/guides/how-to-set-up-a-new-merchant#guided-setup-for-jvzoo-clickbank-digistore24-buygoods-and-explodely). The steps below follow the guided setup. In the merchant's full editor (**Open full editor**) the same fields are on **General information**.
</Note>


1. Go to **Settings** → **Merchants** and click **Create Merchant**
2. Pick **Digistore24** as the **Gateway**, type a **Name**, and click **Create & Configure**
3. **Digistore24 vendor ID**: your Digistore24 user name or vendor ID, at the top right once you log in to Digistore24
4. **IPN passphrase (optional)**: the **IPN password** on your Digistore24 IPN connection, under **Settings** → **Integrations (IPN) configuration**. It must match exactly. Leave it empty if your connection has none (see Step 4).
5. Click **Save and continue**. **Products** opens next.

<img src="/images/merchants/digistore24-setup-account.png" alt="The guided setup of a Digistore24 merchant on Account details: the Digistore24 vendor ID and the optional IPN passphrase, each with where to find it" />

## Step 2: Assign Merchant to Domain

1. Go to **Settings** → **Domains** → **Edit Domain**
2. Select your Digistore24 merchant
3. Save

## Step 3: Map Products

1. Go to **Settings** → **Products** → **Edit**
2. Open the **Merchant information** tab
3. Enter the **Digistore24 Product ID** for this product
4. Save

## Links and Funnels

Use `[BUY]`, `[UPSELL]`, and `[DOWNSELL]` tags so links auto-adapt per domain and merchant. Avoid pasting hardcoded Digistore24 checkout URLs.

If you do paste a Digistore24 `/product/` link, ElasticFunnels adds the visitor's click ID (`ds24tr`) when the page loads, so the sale still matches its visit. A `ds24tr` you set yourself is kept. To leave pasted links exactly as written, turn off **Add click tracking to pasted buy links** in the merchant's **Settings**.

## Step 4: Send orders to ElasticFunnels (IPN connection)

Digistore24 tells ElasticFunnels about each sale, refund and chargeback through an IPN connection. Until one is set up and active, no Digistore24 sale shows up in your reports.

In the guided setup, this is the **Postback URL** step: the postback URL with **Copy**, and the steps for Digistore24.

<img src="/images/merchants/digistore24-setup-postback.png" alt="The Postback URL step of the guided Digistore24 setup: the postback URL with Copy and the steps for the IPN connection in Digistore24" />

You can open the same URL later: click **Postback Setup** on the merchant's row in **Settings** → **Merchants**. It looks like `https://app.elasticfunnels.io/postback/vendor/<your code>/digistore24`.

In Digistore24:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Integrations (IPN) configuration** and click **Add new connection**. If an ElasticFunnels connection is already listed, click its pencil icon to edit it instead.

<img src="/images/merchants/digistore24-ipn-list.png" alt="Digistore24 Settings, Integrations (IPN) configuration with Add new connection and an active ElasticFunnels connection" />

2. Scroll to **For pros**, at the bottom of the list, and pick **Generic**

<img src="/images/merchants/digistore24-ipn-generic.png" alt="Digistore24 connection types with Generic under For pros" />

3. **Name**: anything you will recognize, for example ElasticFunnels. **For products**: tick **all**, or every product you sell through ElasticFunnels.

<img src="/images/merchants/digistore24-ipn-name.png" alt="Digistore24 Generic connection with the name ElasticFunnels and For products set to all" />

4. **Send notifications for**: keep **Orders by customers** ticked
5. **For order event**: tick **payment**, **refund** and **chargeback**. If you sell subscriptions, also tick **missed recurring payment**, **rebilling canceled** and **rebilling resumed**. You can leave **end of paid period** ticked or untick it: ElasticFunnels answers it with OK and does not record it.

<img src="/images/merchants/digistore24-ipn-events.png" alt="Digistore24 Send notifications for and For order event with payment, refund and chargeback ticked" />

6. **IPN URL**: paste the ElasticFunnels URL. Keep **Request method** on POST and **Success validation** on the default (expects text: OK).
7. **IPN password**: optional. If you set one here, type the same value into **IPN passphrase** on the merchant in ElasticFunnels. If you set one in ElasticFunnels only, every IPN is refused.
8. **Active**: **Yes**
9. **Email address for error notifications**: your email, so Digistore24 tells you when an IPN fails
10. Click **Save**

<img src="/images/merchants/digistore24-ipn-url.png" alt="Digistore24 Generic connection with the ElasticFunnels IPN URL, IPN password, Active, Save and Test connection" />

Back on **Integrations (IPN) configuration**, the connection must show a green tick under **Active**.


## Step 5: Test and check that it arrived

1. In ElasticFunnels, click **Postback Setup** on the merchant's row and go to **Test**. Keep the window open.
2. In Digistore24, open the connection and click **Test connection**
3. Within a few seconds the window shows **Merchant verified**, and Digistore24 reports the test as successful. If ElasticFunnels shows **No test postback yet** after 2 minutes, check the URL and click **Check again**.
4. Open **Settings** → **Logs** → **Merchant Postbacks**. The test is listed with merchant Digistore24 and **Response Code** 200. Click **Show Details** to see what Digistore24 sent.

The connection test marks the merchant verified but adds no order to your reports. To see a real order go through, buy from your live page and refund it.

## Sales stopped showing up

Start at **Settings** → **Logs** → **Merchant Postbacks**, filtered to Digistore24.

- **Nothing from Digistore24 since a certain date**: the connection was switched off, deleted or changed. In Digistore24, check that it is listed under **Integrations (IPN) configuration** with a green tick under **Active**, and that its IPN URL matches **Postback Setup**. The merchant still says **Verified**, because that badge only records the first postback. When the daily check sees buy clicks but no sales, it adds an amber **No sales reported since** warning next to the merchant in **Merchants**; hover it to see the clicks and sales it counted. The warning clears with the next postback that arrives.
- **Response Code 400, "Invalid Digistore24 signature"**: the **IPN password** in Digistore24 and the **IPN passphrase** on the merchant differ. Make them the same, or clear both.
- **Only some products arrive**: **For products** is not set to **all** and a product is missing from the list.
- **Orders arrive without a page or ad**: the buy link had no click ID. Use `[BUY]` tags. A Digistore24 `/product/` link pasted into a page also gets the visitor's click ID (`ds24tr`) added when the page loads, unless **Add click tracking to pasted buy links** is off in the merchant's **Settings**.

## Other problems

- Wrong gateway on checkout: Verify the domain is assigned to Digistore24
- Link not working: Ensure product mapping has the correct Digistore24 Product ID
- Missing affiliate data: Confirm affiliate sync settings and use adaptive tags
