The **Test (sandbox)** merchant is a merchant with no credentials that never charges anyone. Use it to try a funnel from the first page to the thank-you page before you connect a real payment processor: the checkout, order bumps, one-click upsells and downsells, course access, order emails, automations, subscription renewals and refunds all work.

Every order it takes is a **test order**. You pay with test card numbers, and only test card numbers: a real card is refused.

## Create a Test merchant

1. Go to **Settings** → **Merchants** → **Create Merchant**.
2. In **Gateway**, pick **Test (sandbox)** and give it a name.
3. Click **Create & Configure**.
4. Pick the **Checkout Page** your funnel uses and click **Save**.

There are no keys to enter. The yellow box on the merchant shows the test cards and what runs for a test order.

Then make it the merchant of the domain or funnel you are testing (**Settings** → **Domains**, or the funnel's merchant setting), the same way you would pick Stripe or NMI.

<Warning>
Use the Test merchant only on domains and funnels you are testing. Anyone who reaches a checkout that uses it can finish the order with a test card and get what it sells for free, including course access. Switch the domain or funnel to your real merchant before you send traffic.
</Warning>

## Test cards

Enter any future expiry date (for example `12/30`) and any CVC.

| Card number | Result |
|---|---|
| `4242 4242 4242 4242` | Approved |
| `4000 0000 0000 0002` | Declined: "Your card was declined." |
| `4000 0000 0000 9995` | Declined: insufficient funds |
| `4000 0000 0000 0069` | Declined: expired card |

The other numbers from Stripe's test card list work too and give the same result as on Stripe (for example `5555 5555 5555 4444` is an approved Mastercard). Any other card number is refused before anything is recorded, and an expiry date in the past is refused.

## What a test order does

A test order made through the Test merchant runs the same steps as a paid order, so you can check the whole funnel:

- the thank-you page, the order bump and the order total;
- one-click upsells and downsells, charged to the test card saved at checkout;
- course access for the products bought, and the welcome and order emails;
- automations that start on a purchase;
- subscriptions: the renewal charges the saved test card on its date, and the renewal emails go out;
- refunds and voids from the order page.

Some steps never run for it, because nothing was paid:

- it is left out of revenue, analytics and reports, like any test order;
- nothing is sent to a fulfillment provider;
- no affiliate postback, no ad platform conversion (Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and the others) and no tracker postback;
- no sale is sent to a call center, no stock is taken and no processing fee is charged.

Your merchant's own [postback URLs](/merchants/postback-urls) still fire, with `"test": true` in the payload.

## Moving to a real processor

When the funnel works, create your real merchant (Stripe, NMI, Authorize.Net or another) and switch the domain or funnel to it. To keep testing on a live merchant afterwards, use its **Test Key**.
