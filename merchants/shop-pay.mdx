This guide explains how to set up Shop Pay as a merchant. When a visitor clicks a buy link, ElasticFunnels creates a draft order in Shopify and sends them to Shopify checkout. Returning customers can use Shop Pay one-click. The overall flow matches the generic merchant setup; use this page for Shop Pay-specific notes.

<Note>
**Shop Pay** uses Shopify's Draft Order API and supports bumps (multiple line items). For a simple cart redirect to your Shopify store, see [Shopify Checkout](/merchants/shopify-checkout) instead.
</Note>

## Prerequisites
{/* SCREENSHOT: Prerequisites · merchant setup screen or postback state · desktop */}

- A Shopify store (same connection as Order Sync). One store per brand
- Admin access to your Shopify store and to ElasticFunnels
- Your Shopify store is connected to the brand (see [Shopify Order Sync](/integrations/shopify-order-sync#set-up))

## Step 1: Connect Shopify and enable Shop Pay
{/* SCREENSHOT: Step 1: Connect Shopify and enable Shop Pay · merchant setup screen or postback state · desktop */}

Shop Pay requires your Shopify store to be connected so ElasticFunnels can create draft orders.

1. Go to **Integrations** → **Operations**, find **Shopify**, and connect your store (see [Shopify Order Sync](/integrations/shopify-order-sync) for the full flow)
2. When connecting (or in the integration settings), enable **Shop Pay** so the integration can create draft orders

When you enable **Shop Pay**, a **Shop Pay** merchant is created automatically and linked to the integration. You do not need to create the merchant manually.

## Step 2: Assign merchant to domain
{/* SCREENSHOT: Step 2: Assign merchant to domain · merchant setup screen or postback state · desktop */}

1. Go to **Settings** → **Domains** → **Edit Domain**
2. Select your **Shop Pay** merchant (created in Step 1)
3. Save

## Step 3: Map products
{/* SCREENSHOT: Step 3: Map products · merchant setup screen or postback state · desktop */}

Each product must be linked to a **Shopify variant ID** (a numeric ID from your Shopify store).

<Note>
If **Enable Shop Pay Merchant** was on when you connected the store, ElasticFunnels creates your products in Shopify (one variant each, inventory not tracked) and fills in the variant ID for you; later edits to a product are sent to Shopify too. With **Sync orders to Shopify** on, the **Sync products** button on the Shopify card does the same for products not in Shopify yet. Enter a variant ID by hand when the field is still empty, or to use a product that already exists in your Shopify store.
</Note>

To enter it by hand:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Products** → **Edit** the product
2. Open the **Merchant information** tab
3. Find the row for your **Shop Pay** merchant
4. Enter the **Shopify variant ID** (numbers only) for this product
5. Save

### How to find your Shopify variant ID

- **Products with variants:** In Shopify Admin go to **Products** → open the product → **Variants** → click the variant. The URL in your browser will look like `.../variants/36485954240671`. The number after `/variants/` is the variant ID.
- **Products with a single variant:** Open the product in Shopify Admin, add `.json` to the end of the URL, and press Enter. In the JSON, look at the `variants` array and use the `id` of the first (or only) variant.

<Info>
Every Shopify product has at least one variant. Use the variant ID, not the product ID.
</Info>

## How it works
{/* SCREENSHOT: How it works · merchant setup screen or postback state · desktop */}

When a visitor clicks a buy link on your funnel:

- A **draft order** is created in your Shopify store with the selected product (and any bump products)
- The customer is redirected to Shopify checkout to complete payment
- Returning customers can use **Shop Pay** one-click checkout when available
- Bumps are supported. Multiple line items can be added to the same draft order
- When the order is paid, Shopify notifies ElasticFunnels and the sale is recorded with its click ID, products and bonuses. Refunds are linked to the sale
- There are no one-click upsells: payment happens on Shopify, so each purchase goes through Shopify checkout
- These orders already exist in Shopify, so [Shopify Order Sync](/integrations/shopify-order-sync) does not send them again

There is no “decline” link; if the customer does not complete checkout, they simply leave. Use **Postback Setup** on the merchant's row in **Settings** → **Merchants** for postback configuration and validation.

## Postbacks
{/* SCREENSHOT: Postbacks · merchant setup screen or postback state · desktop */}

Click **Postback Setup** on the merchant's row in **Settings** → **Merchants**. Its steps are listed on the left: **Postback URL** (your unique URL, with **Copy**), then the setup steps for Shopify, then **Test**. After you configure the postback and trigger a test (or complete a test purchase), keep it open on **Test**: it checks for the postback every 5 seconds for 2 minutes and marks the merchant verified as soon as the first one arrives.

## Troubleshooting
{/* SCREENSHOT: Troubleshooting · merchant setup screen or postback state · desktop */}

- **"Product missing Shopify product ID"** or **"missing Shopify product ID"**: In the product's **Merchant information** tab, enter the Shopify variant ID for the Shop Pay merchant. Use the steps above to find the variant ID in Shopify Admin.
- **"Shop Pay gateway requires a Shopify integration"** or **"requires a Shopify store domain"**: Connect Shopify under **Integrations** → **Operations** and turn on **Enable Shop Pay Merchant** on the Shopify card, so the Shop Pay merchant is created and linked to your store.
- **Wrong gateway on checkout**: Verify the domain is assigned to your Shop Pay merchant.
- **Link not working**: Confirm the product has the correct Shopify variant ID in **Merchant information** for this merchant.
