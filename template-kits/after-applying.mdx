A kit arrives as drafts. Visitors cannot see any of it until you publish the pages and connect a domain. This page lists where each part lands and the checks to make before you go live.

## Where everything lands

| Part | Where to find it | State after apply |
|---|---|---|
| Pages | **Pages** | Draft, no domain, at the URLs shown on the review |
| Funnel | **Funnels** | A new funnel, with no domain. It is never made your default funnel |
| Products | **Products** | New products under the names and codes you chose, or the products you linked |
| Page events | On each page's **Page events** | New event flows that point at the new pages and products |
| Components | **Pages** > **Components** | New components, or yours where the kit reused them |
| Variables | Your project variables | New variables only. Variables you had keep their value |
| Files | Your project's files | Copied in the background right after the apply |
| Quizzes | **Quizzes** | A draft quiz on its kit page. Publish it from the quiz builder |

Applying a kit never edits or deletes your existing pages, funnels or products. The one exception is a course portal: when a kit's members pages land on course pages that the project created for you and you never edited, the kit replaces them and keeps the old version as a revision.

## Check the kit before you publish

Work through these steps once for each kit you apply.

### 1. Find the kit pages

1. Open **Pages**.
2. Find the kit pages at the top of the list. Each one is marked **Draft**, and the **Funnels** column shows the kit's funnel.

<img src="/images/template-kits/after-pages.png" alt="The Pages list after applying a kit: the kit pages at the top, each marked Draft, with the kit funnel in the Funnels column" />

### 2. Open the funnel

1. Open **Funnels** and click the kit's funnel.
2. Check the path from **Funnel Entry Point** through the entry page, **On Purchase** and the upsells.
3. Connect a domain to the funnel and publish it when the pages are ready.

<img src="/images/template-kits/after-funnel.png" alt="The funnel builder showing the funnel the kit created: Funnel Entry Point, the entry page group with the product page, On Purchase and the first upsell" />

See [Create a funnel](/funnels/create-funnel) for the funnel builder.

### 3. Check each product's titles

1. Open **Products** and click a product the kit created.
2. Check **Code** and **Title**. They are the ones you typed on the review.
3. Change **Checkout Title**. It still holds the kit's wording, and it is the name customers see on the checkout.
4. Set the price, and the cost of goods if you report on profit.
5. Click **Save**.

<img src="/images/template-kits/after-product.png" alt="A product the kit created: its Code and Title are the ones typed on the review, and the Checkout Title still holds the kit's wording" />

### 4. Check the stock

Products created by a kit start with **Stock Quantity** blank. A blank box means stock is not tracked: the product is always in stock and sells without counting.

1. On the same product, scroll to **Inventory**.
2. If you do not track stock, leave **Stock Quantity** blank.
3. If you do track stock, type the number you have, then pick the **Out-of-Stock Policy**: **Stop selling** shows the product as sold out at 0 and no new checkout is charged for it, **Continue selling** keeps taking orders. A paid order is never cancelled for stock (see [How stock moves](/products/product-management#how-stock-moves)). The policy only applies once a quantity is set.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Repeat for every product and variant the kit created that you want to track, including free gifts.

<img src="/images/template-kits/after-stock.png" alt="The Inventory section of a product the kit created: Stock Quantity is blank, so the product sells without tracking stock" />

### 5. Connect your merchant and fulfillment

Kit products arrive with no merchant product IDs, no fulfillment integration, no category, no shipping profile and no folder. Set the ones you use:

1. On each product, add your merchant's product ID in the merchant tab if your merchant needs one. See [Set up a new merchant](/guides/how-to-set-up-a-new-merchant).
2. Pick your fulfillment integration and shipping profile if you ship physical goods.

If your project has no merchant yet, applying a kit with a checkout page adds the Test merchant (**Test (sandbox)**) to that checkout and sets it on your free `*.elasticpages.co` subdomain, so you can place a test order right away. Orders through it are test orders and nothing is charged: add your real merchant under **Settings** > **Merchants** and set it on your domain before you send traffic.

### 6. Check the page events

Open each page's page events. If the apply panel said **Check these before you publish**, a step that chose a merchant or an automation in the kit was cleared, because those belong to the kit's own project. Pick yours in that step.

### 7. Replace the kit's copy and images

The pages still carry the kit's sample product, brand and claims. Edit each page in the editor or the code editor, and replace the images with your own. [Kit images](/template-kits/kit-images) shows how to make your own version of each kit image from its prompt.

A kit page that carries a quiz shows the quiz. Open the quiz from **Quizzes**, change its questions, answers and links if you need to, and publish it from the builder before you publish its page. See [Build and publish a quiz](/quizzes/build-and-publish).

If you apply the same kit again at the same URLs, the quiz you already have is kept with your edits; no second copy is made. Applying it at other URLs creates new quizzes for the new pages.

#### The gut health quiz kit

The **Quiz funnel for a gut-health weight-loss supplement** kit installs its quiz as a quiz builder quiz. When you apply it, your project gets:

- its own copy of the 19-question quiz, saved as a **Draft** under **Quizzes** and linked to the kit's quiz page;
- quiz leads sent to the collection the kit creates in your project, so entries land in your CRM and not in the kit's;
- your brand name in the quiz text where the template had its sample name. If your project has no `brand_name` variable yet, it starts as your project's name. Your own values for `product_name`, `company_name` and `support_email` replace the template's sample values in the quiz the same way.

To edit it, open **Quizzes**, open the quiz and change any screen, answer or result card in the builder. The visitor first sees the quiz page's intro, where they pick a gender, and the quiz then opens over it. The result sends them to the plan page with a 1, 3 or 6 month plan picked from the weight they want to lose. After a purchase, the thank-you page moves the quiz answers onto the buyer's customer record.

Publish the quiz from the builder, then publish its page. Two things to set yourself:

- The landing and plan pages keep the kit's sample product and company names until you set the `product_name` and `company_name` variables.
- The probiotic quiz kit still ships a hand-built quiz page, because its result screen shows live prices and adds to the cart, which the quiz builder cannot do yet. Edit that quiz in the page editor.

### 8. Publish

Publish each page when it is ready, and connect a domain. See [Create a landing page](/pages/create-landing-page) and [Page settings](/pages/page-settings).
