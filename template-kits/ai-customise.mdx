After you apply a kit, its pages still sell the kit's sample product. **Customise with AI** rewrites them for yours: it reads your brief, your product facts and your pictures, and changes the copy, product names, prices and images on every kit page. The pages stay unpublished until you review them.

## What the AI uses

| You give | Used for |
|---|---|
| A brief: text you type, or one file (`.txt`, `.md`, `.pdf`, `.docx`, up to 10 MB) | Headlines, claims, testimonials, numbers and the voice of the pages |
| Product facts | Product names, prices, guarantee, ingredients, audience, which claims are allowed |
| A Google Drive folder link (optional) | Your pictures and documents, read from the folder |
| Product pictures | Packshots, labels, logos, lifestyle photos, testimonials, certificates |

Your copy goes in as you wrote it. The AI does not soften, remove or fact-check your headlines, claims, testimonials or numbers; you are responsible for them. Where your brief has nothing for a section, the AI writes it from the brief and the kit.

## Add your brief and pictures

1. Apply the kit as usual (see [Apply a kit](/template-kits/apply-a-kit)).
2. After the products step, type your brief in the brief box, or attach one file.
3. Optional: paste the link to a Google Drive folder. The folder must be shared with **Anyone with the link**; ElasticFunnels does not sign in to Google, and a private folder shows an error asking you to share it.
4. Upload your product pictures, and for each one pick the product and what it shows: packshot, label, logo, lifestyle, reference, testimonial or certificate.

From a Drive folder the AI reads images (JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF), documents (PDF, DOCX, Markdown, text), spreadsheets (CSV, XLSX) and Google Docs, Sheets and Slides. Other files are listed as skipped.

## See what is missing

The **missing** list shows what the AI will not have:

- a kit product with no packshot;
- an image slot that still has no image;
- facts the kit pages need that your brief does not give, such as the price, guarantee days or ingredients;
- Drive files that could not be read.

Each row says which pages it affects and how to fix it: upload a picture, generate an image, or answer the question. Missing items do not stop the AI. The one exception is a Drive folder that has not finished loading.

## Start it

1. Leave **Customise with AI** on. It is on by default when you add a brief or a Drive link.
2. Check the model tier. **Balanced** is the default.
3. Read the credit estimate. It is a range, for example about 180 to 260 credits: the pages to rewrite, plus each image slot that still needs an image.
4. Start it.

The job holds the top of the range while it runs and stops if it reaches it. Whatever it does not use goes back to your balance.

## What the AI changes

- **Products:** renames and reprices the kit products from your facts. Product codes stay the ones you chose on the review.
- **Pages:** rewrites the copy on each kit page and swaps product names, prices, guarantee and images. It keeps the kit's layout and its buy links, checkout and template tags.
- **Images:** your own pictures first. For a slot with no picture, it generates one from the kit prompt, within the credit estimate.
- **Publishing:** nothing. The pages stay unpublished.

When it finishes, it lists what it changed on each page and what is still missing.

## Review and publish

1. Open each kit page and read it.
2. Fix anything you want to word differently, in the editor or by asking the AI editor.
3. Check the stock and titles of each product (see [After applying](/template-kits/after-applying)).
4. Publish the pages.

## Later chats in the AI editor

The AI editor knows when a project came from a kit. In later chats it reads the kit, your brief and your facts before it changes kit copy or images, so you do not have to explain them again.
