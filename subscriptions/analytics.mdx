Subscription analytics live in your dashboards. There is no separate Subscription Analytics page: you build a dashboard from the **Subscriptions** preset, or add the subscription cards to any dashboard you already have.

## Create the Subscriptions dashboard
{/* SCREENSHOT: Dashboard → Create dashboard → preset picker with Subscriptions selected · desktop */}

1. Open **Dashboard** and click **Create dashboard**.
2. Choose **Start from a preset** and pick **Subscriptions**.
3. Save. The dashboard opens with the KPI tiles and cards described below.

To add a single card to another dashboard, open the dashboard builder and pick it from the **Subscriptions** category.

## Cards

| Card | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **MRR Trend** | Subscription revenue collected per month, so you can see growth and seasonal dips. |
| **Churn vs New Subscribers** | New subscriptions and cancellations per month. When cancellations stay above new subscriptions, your base is shrinking. |
| **Cohort LTV** | Cumulative revenue per acquisition month over 12 months. Each row is the group of customers who first subscribed that month. |
| **Dunning Recovery** | Subscriptions saved by failed-payment retries, subscriptions lost, and the recovery rate. |
| **Payment Health** | Renewal fail rate, recovery rate and chargeback ratio. |
| **Retention Curves** | The share of each cohort still subscribed month by month. |
| **Decline Reasons** | Why renewal charges were declined. |
| **Cancel Reasons** | The reasons buyers and agents gave when they canceled, from the members area and the Call Center cancel flow. |
| **Net Revenue Retention** | NRR over time, showing expansion against contraction. |
| **Subscription KPIs** | Active subscriptions, MRR, churn rate and average LTV in one card. |

<Tip>
Cohort LTV shows retention problems earlier than the churn rate does. If one cohort's revenue flattens sooner than the others, look at what changed in your offer or onboarding that month.
</Tip>

<Info>
To tune the failed-payment retries behind Dunning Recovery, see [Subscription Emails & Dunning](/subscriptions/emails-and-dunning).
</Info>

## Filters

The dashboard's date range applies to every card, and the merchant and product filters narrow the subscription figures to one merchant or product.
