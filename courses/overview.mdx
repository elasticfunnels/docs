A course is a set of **modules** (a week, a chapter) that hold **lessons**. You sell it with the
products, checkouts and funnels you already use: link a product to the course, and everyone who
buys that product is enrolled as soon as the payment goes through.

Courses is not in the sidebar for now. To see your courses, filter drafts and create a new one,
open `/{brand}/courses` in the app, where `{brand}` is the number in your app address (for example
`app.elasticfunnels.io/123/courses`). A product's **Courses** tab lists the courses it unlocks.

## Create a course
{/* SCREENSHOT: New course · the "Where will students take this course?" choice above the name and summary fields · desktop */}

Click **New course**. The first question is **Where will students take this course?**

| Choice | What you get |
|---|---|
| **ElasticFunnels course pages** | A course library, a course page and a lesson player on your domain, ready to use. You can change the design later. See [Course pages](/courses/student-pages). |
| **My own pages or app** | No course pages are created. Your members area, your own pages or your app read the course with the course functions, and the course screen shows where it is used and the code to paste. See [Courses on your own pages](/courses/own-pages). |

Then give the course a name and a short summary of what students will learn. Instructor,
category and a cover image are optional. A project with no domain yet starts on your own pages;
add a domain to use the course pages.

You can switch between the two later in the course's **Settings**. Students, lessons and access
stay as they are when you switch.

## The setup checklist
{/* SCREENSHOT: Course Overview · "Get your course ready" checklist with five steps · desktop */}

A new course opens on its **Overview**, with a checklist called **Get your course ready**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the course">
    A name and a short summary are what students see first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add lessons">
    Group lessons into modules on the **Curriculum** tab. See [Lessons](#lessons) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what unlocks it">
    Link the products that enrol their buyers, or make the course free. See [Access](#access).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview as a student, or use it in your pages">
    On course pages, **Preview as student** opens the course the way an enrolled student sees it,
    drafts included. On your own pages, this step shows the code to paste.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Students can open the course once it is published.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Preview as student** signs you in as a preview student of your own account through a one-time
link that is valid for 15 minutes. No welcome email is sent, and the preview shows on the
**Students** tab with a **Preview** badge. A preview is not counted in the course's number of
students.

## Lessons
{/* SCREENSHOT: Curriculum tab · outline on the left, lesson editor with Featured media and the lesson body · desktop */}

On the **Curriculum** tab, the outline lists modules and their lessons. Drag to reorder them, and
select a module or lesson to edit it next to the outline. A module or lesson needs a title; its
description is optional.

A lesson has:

- **Featured media** (optional): a **Video** (a YouTube, Vimeo, Loom or Bunny link, an uploaded
  video file, or an embed code) or an **Audio** file or link with a cover image. Choose **None**
  for a written lesson, such as a short essay, a step guide or a file pack.
- **Lesson**: the body. Add images, numbered steps, a key details box, videos and embeds
  (YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Wistia, Bunny Stream, Miro) anywhere in the text.
- **Files**: downloads under the lesson, such as worksheets, transcripts and templates.
- **Key takeaways**: one line per thing to remember or do.
- **Comments**: follow the course setting, or turn them on or off for this lesson.

## Access
{/* SCREENSHOT: What unlocks this course · product list with Paid / Free choice · desktop */}

Click **Link a product** (or **Change** under **Unlocked by**) to choose what unlocks the course:

- **Paid**: only buyers of a linked product and students you add get in. A purchase of a linked
  product enrols the buyer, including when the product is bought as an upsell.
- **Free**: any signed-in visitor can enrol in the published course. Linked products still enrol
  their buyers.

You can link several products to one course, for example a bundle and a standalone product. The
same list is on each product, on its **Courses** tab.

Access follows the money:

| Event | What happens to course access |
|---|---|
| Refund or chargeback | Access to the courses that sale unlocked ends right away. |
| Subscription cancelled | Access stays until the paid period ends (the default). |
| Subscription resumed | Access comes back. |

The subscription rule is a project setting with three choices: keep course access until the paid
period ends, end course access right away, or keep course access. It sits in the project's course
settings at `/{brand}/settings?tab=courses`, under **When a subscription is cancelled**. Refunds and
chargebacks always end access, whatever this setting says.

## Students
{/* SCREENSHOT: Students tab · list with progress, status and source · desktop */}

The **Students** tab lists everyone with access, with search and paging. Each student shows their
progress, when they enrolled and their status: **Active**, **Revoked** (with the reason: refunded,
chargeback, subscription cancelled or revoked by an admin) or **Expired**.

- **Add student** gives someone access without a purchase, for example a partner or a past
  customer. They get the course welcome email with a sign-in link. Adding a revoked or expired
  student again reopens their access.
- **Revoke access** stops a student's access and keeps them on the list. **Restore access** gives
  it back.
- **Remove from course** deletes the student and their progress.

Open a student to see their progress, how they got access (purchase, upsell, added by your team,
import or free enrollment), the order, and private notes that only your team sees.

## Student emails
{/* SCREENSHOT: Settings → Student emails · welcome email picker with Edit, Send test to me and Duplicate for this course · desktop */}

Every new student gets one welcome email. In the course's **Settings → Student emails**:

- **Welcome email** lists the course templates of your project: the two built-in ones (Course
  Enrollment and Course Magic Link) and their copies. Pick one, then **Edit** it, **Send test to
  me**, or **Duplicate for this course** to give this course its own copy.
- **Emails a student can get** lists the welcome and sign-in link email, the sign-in code and the
  reply to a comment, with the template each one uses.

Students sign in to courses the same way customers sign in to your members area (**Members area,
Login method**). With email only or magic link, the welcome email carries the student's sign-in
link. With a code or an order ID, it links to the sign-in page. See [Course emails](/emails/course-emails).

<Note>
The welcome email goes to new enrollments only. Students enrolled more than 7 days ago are not
emailed again.
</Note>

## Delete a course

**Delete course** (in the course's menu on the list) asks you to confirm. Students lose access and
the course leaves your list; their enrollments stay on record as revoked. If the course has its
own pages (see [Customize for this course](/courses/student-pages#customize-for-this-course)), the
dialog offers **Also delete this course's custom pages**. Your shared course design is not touched.
