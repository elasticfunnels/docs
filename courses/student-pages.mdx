A course created with **ElasticFunnels course pages** runs on three pages that ElasticFunnels
creates for your project the first time it needs them:

| Page | What students see |
|---|---|
| **Course library** | The student's courses, each with its progress and where to pick up |
| **Course page** | The course home: the description, how far the student is, the next lesson with a **Start the first lesson** or **Continue the course** button, the instructor, and every module with its lessons and their done marks |
| **Lesson player** | One lesson: the lesson list on the side with the current one marked, its video or audio, the written lesson, files, **Mark as complete**, the previous and next lesson, and comments |

They are ordinary pages, filed in a page folder called **Courses** so they stay out of the way in
your Pages list. Edit them in the page editor like any other page. These three pages are your
**default course design**: every course without a design of its own uses them.

Opening the course link takes a signed-in student to the course page, not straight into a lesson.

If a course says **Course pages are not set up yet**, click **Set up course pages** in its
**Settings → Student pages**. It takes a second.

## Where students open the course
{/* SCREENSHOT: Course Overview · "Where students find it" card with the course link and Copy link · desktop */}

The course **Overview** shows **Where students find it**: the course's address on your domain,
with **Copy link**. A published course opens there for enrolled students, and the welcome email
links to it. A draft opens only for you, with **Preview as student**.

The address is the project's course domain and path, then the course's slug, for example
`https://yourdomain.com/course/sleep-reset`. Lessons open under it at
`/course/sleep-reset/module/{module}/lesson/{lesson}`. Change the domain and the course slug in
the course's **Settings** under **Course address**.

## Customize for this course
{/* SCREENSHOT: Settings → Student pages · "Using your default course design" with Customize for this course and the three pages · desktop */}

Open the course's **Settings → Student pages**. A course on the default design shows **Using your
default course design** and the three shared pages, each with **Edit page**.

<Warning>
Editing a shared page changes it for every course that uses the default design.
</Warning>

To give one course its own look, click **Customize for this course**. ElasticFunnels copies the
course page and the lesson player into a new page folder named **Course: &lt;course title&gt;**,
points this course at the copies and opens the editor in a new tab. Changes to the copies affect
only this course. The course library stays shared.

- **Reset to default** puts the course back on the shared design. The copies stay in their
  folder, so you can switch back later.
- Renaming the course renames its folder too.
- If a copy is deleted or taken offline, the course screen shows a warning and students see the
  default design instead of an error.
- Deleting the course offers **Also delete this course's custom pages**, which removes the pages
  in its own folder. The shared pages are never deleted with a course.

## Lesson content on your pages

The lesson editor writes plain HTML that the lesson player styles. If you build your own lesson
page, these are the blocks it can contain:

| Block | Markup |
|---|---|
| Key details box | `div.ocp-callout` with a `p.ocp-callout-t` title |
| Numbered steps | `ol.ocp-steps > li` |
| Embed (YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, Wistia, Bunny Stream, Miro) | `div.crs-embed > iframe` |
| Uploaded video | `video.crs-video` |

The default lesson player includes styles for all four. A course on [your own
pages](/courses/own-pages) gets the same styles as a snippet to copy.
