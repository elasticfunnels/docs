**Send email** sends one email from an automation through a sending account you pick. It works
with every kind of account your brand has, so one step covers SMTP, Amazon SES, Postmark, Brevo,
Resend, Mailgun, SendGrid and Mailrelay. Find it in the step list under **Email**.

<img src="/images/automations/send-email-step.png" alt="The Send email step's options: Sending account, Email template, To, Subject, Body with variables, and the optional From email with its verification notice" />

## The fields

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sending account** | The account that sends it. The list shows each active account as provider, name and its default from address, for example `Postmark: Acme receipts (orders@example.com)`. Paused accounts are not listed. |
| **Email template** | Optional. Picking a saved email copies its subject, body, from address, from name and reply-to into the step. You can still change them in the step. **Create new email** makes a template, and **Edit email in builder** opens the picked one in a new tab. |
| **To** | Required. An address, or a variable such as the customer's email from the trigger. |
| **Subject** | Required. Variables work here. |
| **Body (HTML)** | The email itself. Variables from earlier steps work here. **Expand** gives you a bigger editor. |
| **From email**, **From name**, **Reply-to email** | Optional, under the extra fields. Leave **From email** empty to send as the account's default sender. |

Variables are picked the same way as in any other step. See [Variables](/automations/variables).

## Which account sends it

With **Sending account** picked, that account sends the email. Leave it empty and the step uses:

1. your brand SMTP server, when the brand has one,
2. otherwise your brand's email sending account, when it has exactly one.

With no SMTP and several sending accounts, pick one in the step, or the email is not sent.

An account must belong to the brand and be active. A paused account is refused, and the step
fails instead of sending through another account.

## Which sender it uses

A **From email** typed in the step wins over the account's default sender. It has to be an
address the provider accepts: on a domain or sender you verified with that provider, or the
provider refuses the email. The notice above the field says the same.

With **From email** empty, the account's **Default From Email** is used. The full rule is on
[How the sender is chosen](/emails/sending-accounts#how-the-sender-is-chosen).

Picking an email template fills **From email** with the template's from address. Clear the field
if you want the account's default sender instead.

## When it fails

If the provider refuses the email (an unverified from address, a revoked key) or no account can
send it, the step fails and the provider's reply is shown on the run. See
[Run history](/automations/run-history).

## The older per-provider steps

The **Mailgun**, **SendGrid** and **Mailrelay** send steps are still in the step list and keep
working in the automations that use them. They only send through their own provider. Use
**Send email** for Amazon SES, Postmark, Brevo, Resend or your SMTP server, or when you want to
switch providers later by changing one field.
