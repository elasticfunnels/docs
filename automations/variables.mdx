Every step in an automation can use what the steps before it returned: the buyer's email from the trigger, the
result of an email check, the ID of the contact a CRM step created. You pick these fields with the variable
picker in the step's options.

<img src="/images/automations/variables-untested-step.png" alt="A Continue If step's variable picker listing the Valid, Validation Result and Message fields of a DeBounce Validate Email step that has never been tested" />

## Pick a field

1. In the automation builder, click the step you want to set up.
2. On its **Options tab**, click a field (for example **Data** on a **Continue If** step).
3. The picker lists each earlier step with its fields and an example value. Type in the search box to filter by
   name or value.
4. Click a field to use it.

The field then reads as the field's name and its step, for example **Valid · Validate Email**, with an example
value next to it. Click it to change it. Behind the label the step stores a variable such as
`{nwdeb00005.valid}`: the step's code, then the field.

## Steps you have not tested yet

You do not need to test a step before the next one can use its fields. Integration steps come with an example
of what they return, so the picker lists their fields straight away. In the image, the **Validate Email**
(DeBounce) step has never been tested and the **Continue If** after it can already check its **Valid** field.

- The example values are made up (`jane.doe@example.com`, `true`). They show the shape of the data only.
- Once you test a step on its **Test tab**, its real result replaces the example in the picker.
- A test of a later step sends only real tested data to the provider, never the example values.

Steps with an example output include the email platforms (Klaviyo, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, AWeber, Brevo,
Kit, GetResponse, Maropost), email sending (Mailgun, SendGrid, Mailrelay), CRMs (HubSpot, Keap, Agile CRM,
Close, Pipedrive, Ontraport), email checks (DeBounce, ZeroBounce), messaging (Twilio, SlickText, Slack, Discord)
and Google Sheets.

## When a field shows a warning

A field shown in amber with **Not in that step's data** points at a field the step does not return, or at a
step that is no longer in the automation. Pick the field again from the picker. If the step returns different
fields for different inputs, test it once so the picker lists the fields it really returned.
